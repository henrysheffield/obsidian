# A simple life of great focus is the best life possible

> “If you set yourself to your present task along the path of true reason, with all determination, vigour, and good will: if you admit no distraction, but keep your own divinity pure and standing strong … and a heroic truthfulness in all that you say and mean - then you will live a good life. And nobody will be able to stop you.”x


I must follow the “path of true reason” with vigour. Moreover, don’t be afraid of [[Judgement]] do whatever the hell you want and “nobody will be able to stop you.”
