# APOE (Alipoprotein E)

## Notes

Gene that instructs proteins that package transport cholesterol (or other lipids) through the bloodstream to the brain.

Variations of the gene significantly change risk of cardiovascular disease or [[Alzheimer’s]]

| Genotype | Rarity                     | Risk        |
| -------- | -------------------------- | ----------- |
| E3       | most common                | neutral     |
| E2       | rarest                     | lowest risk |
| E4       | somewhat uncommon (10-15%) | major risk  |
> [!NOTE]
> We inherit two copies of APOE allele (maternal, paternal).
> One copy of E4 → 2-3 times higher chance of late Alzheimer’s
> Two copies of E4 → 12 times higher chance
