# Active Listening

## Notes

- Always listen and ask **open-ended questions** to speaker
- Open ended questions cannot be answered as ‘yes’ or ‘no’
- Always ask questions with engagement

**Open ended questions**
- Cannot be answered with *yes or no*
- Typically begin with *how, what or why*
- Encourages people to elaborate

Examples

| **Category**   | **Question**                                                                  | **Why it Works**                                         |
| -------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Daily Life** | "What's been the highlight of your week so far?"                              | Focuses on the positive and opens up recent stories.     |
| **Travel**     | "What's your absolute favorite place to travel, and what made it so special?" | Invites emotional connection and nostalgic descriptions. |
| **Deep Dive**  | "What's one thing most people would be surprised to learn about you?"         | Breaks past surface-level small talk safely.             |
