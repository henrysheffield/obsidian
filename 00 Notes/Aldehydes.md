# Aldehydes

## Notes

Carbon bonded to:
- An oxygen atom (double bond)
- A hydrogen atom (single)
- A functional group (R, single)

### Properties

- Highly reactive
- Suffix: **-al** e.g. butanal
- Small aldehydes are gases with pungent odors (e.g. formaldehyde)
- Large aldehydes have pleasant, fruity aromas
