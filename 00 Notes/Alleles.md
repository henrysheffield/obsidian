# Alleles

## Notes

Different flavours of [[Genes]] (e.g. $B ~\text{or}~b$)

**Can be:**

**Dominant**: 
- Represented by a capital letter ($B$)
- Higher priority
- Generally more common traits (e.g. *brown eyes*)
**Recessive**
- Represented by a lowercase letter ($b$)
- Lower priority
- Generally more rarer traits (e.g. *blue eyes*)

## More specifically:

[[Homo|Homozygous Dominant]]  - Meaning same but dominant → $BB$
[[Homo|Homozygous Recessive]] - Meaning same but recessive → $bb$
[[Hetero|Herterozygous]] - Meaning different → $Bb$ 
→Note: It is always uppercase letter first (e.g. $Ff$)
## Examples / Problems

A [[Gene|gene]] codes for the eye colour trait. An allele codes for what colour of the eye it is (e.g. blue, green, red, etc) [^1]
