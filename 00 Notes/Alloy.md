# Alloy

## Notes

Mixture of 2 or more metals.

Regular metals can sometimes be too soft (e.g. gold) or slide due to the arrangement. Alloys can be synthesized to be more durable and hard for construction / other purposes

![[Alloy-1784353844151.webp]]

Two types:

### 1. Substitutional Alloy

- Metal atoms are of similar size (~15% max difference)
- S for similar
- Bronze 

### 2. Interstitial Alloy

- Metal atoms of significantly different size
- Steel (iron + carbon)
