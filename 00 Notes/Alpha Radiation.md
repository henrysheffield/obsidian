## Alpha Radiation

### Description

**Alpha radiation** ($\alpha$) occurs when an unstable nucleus emits an alpha particle to achieve a more stable state. An alpha particle consists of **two protons and two neutrons**, making it identical to a helium-4 nucleus ($^4_2\text{He}$).

### Physical Properties

- **Composition:** 2 protons + 2 neutrons ($^4_2\text{He}$).
    
- **Mass:** Approximately 4 amu (relatively heavy compared to other radiation types).
    
- **Charge:** $+2$.

## Behaviour and Interaction

- **Ionizing Power:** **High**. Due to its large mass and $+2$ charge, it easily knocks electrons off atoms it encounters.
	→ It has approximately 30,000 - 50,000 ions per cm of air
    
- **Penetrating Power:** **Very Low**. Because of its size and high reactivity, it is easily stopped by a single sheet of paper or the outer layer of human skin.
