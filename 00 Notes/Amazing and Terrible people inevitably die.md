# Amazing and Terrible people inevitably die

> “Hippocrates cured many diseases then died of a disease himself … Alexander, Pompey, Julius Caeser … depart this universe.”


People who did good in the world like *Hippocrates* died a long time ago, this was completely unavoidable and is a powerful reason why [[Should you be afraid of death?|we should not be afraid of death]]
