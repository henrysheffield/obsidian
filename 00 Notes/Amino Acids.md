# Amino Acids

## Notes

![[Amino Acids-1782959330895.webp]]
*Sketch of glycine, note: the dotted wedge means the functional group is facing AWAY from our face*

- 20 Possible amino acids
- Building blocks of [[Proteins]]
- Form peptide bonds (strong, covalent) to connect to each other
## Condensation reaction of glycine + glycine

![[Amino Acids-1782959428731.webp]]
*Condensation (Dehydration–water is lost) reaction between glycine and glycine to form glyglycine*

- Amino acids bond with each other in a **dehydration reaction**
- Water is lost
Can form:
1. Dipeptide (2)
2. Oligopeptide (3 - 10)
3. Polypeptide (>10)
