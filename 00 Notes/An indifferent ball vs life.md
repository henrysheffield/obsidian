# An indifferent ball vs life

>”the ball itself … is actually indifferent … it is what one *does* with the ball that defines the game.”

The ball does not care about who is playing with it or what happens; it is **indifferent**. 

The player can put as much effort into the game as he wants, but even then, it is impossible to control the outcome of the game.
