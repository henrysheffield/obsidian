# Anaesthesia

## Notes

Temporary state of unconsciousness or unawareness of potential pain caused by surgery

### Local anaesthesia

- A specific part of your body is numbed to prevent pain
- You are still awake and fully aware
- You feel pressure but no pain
- Very quick

### Conscious sedation

- You are awake but in a sleepy state and only partly conscious
- Doctor may ask you regular questions
- You will likely not remember procedure

### General anaesthesia

- Strong medicines
- Completely unconscious and unaware of surroundings
- For six hours before surgery, do not eat or drink anything
