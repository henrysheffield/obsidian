**Main Instructions** 


Make a table of sentences in french with english translations for anki. There are two types:
1. Listen: the french sentence is in the first column, and the english translation AND the same french sentence is in the second column + in brackets the mistake i made (e.g. (le poission not la poisson)), in the 3rd column you add “french listen” separated by spaces as tags
2. Produce: The english sentence is in the first column, the french sentence is in the second column (in the second column add the mistake made (e.g. le possion not la possion))
You must synthesise new sentences according to the mistakes I made e.g. if I made a mistake of ‘la poisson’ create a sentence like, Même si le poisson est frais, il sent très mauvais.

### Example Table:

| Column 1                                            | Column 2                                                                                                                                       | Tags           |
| --------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------- |
| Même si le poisson est frais, il sent très mauvais. | Même si le poisson est frais, il sent très mauvais. / Even though the fish is fresh, it smells awful. (Mistake: Use le poisson NOT la poisson) | french listen  |
| Even though the fish is fresh, it smells awful.     | Même si le poisson est frais, il sent très mauvais. (Mistake: Use le poisson NOT la poisson)                                                   | french produce |
### Interpreting the mistake log:

In my mistake log, I have a column for mistakes, a column for correction and also a difficulty number. 

Note: Sometimes the columns may be empty, just synthesise sentences due to one cell’s info.

You must create medium to difficult sentences using each mistake I made.

According to the difficulty, make the corresponding number of flashcards (e.g. difficulty = 3 →3 sentences - 3 flashcards.

### Ratio

Final result should be **50% listen 50% produce**
10% Hard cards (10-20 words)
90% medium cards (no easy cards, instead manageable cards about 5 - 10 words)
