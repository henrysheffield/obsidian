# Annoying everyone at a cinema near you - Plan

## Notes

Time taken: 10 minutes 30s (excluding annotation)



==**Contention**==[^1]: Maddox humorously depicts the rising trend of digital distractions in cinemas to highlight the inconsiderate actions of others in a caracteuruistic manner, which emphasises how they impact the communal enjoyment of film watching.==[^2]

==**P1 Topic Sentence:**==[^4] Maddox ==argues==[^3] that the inconsiderate actions of others ruin the pleasure of visiting the cinema.  

**Improved Topic Sentence**

*Maddox ==humorously exaggerates==[^5] that the inconsiderate actions of others ==illustrate==[^6] how such actions disrupt the communal pleasure of visiting the cinema.*

“she would carefully go to work” - meticulous actions → self absorption
“knitted with a constant click-click-click” - onomatopoeia → auditory irritation
“hissers who rise up like serpents” - simile; dramatise disruption

**P2 Topic Sentence:** ==Maddox warns about the rising trend of digital distractions==[^7]

*Maddox incorportates statistics and a hypothetical scenario to highlight the growing prevalence of digital distractions in cinemas*

statistics → credibility; scale
“55 percent of cinemagoers texted during a movie” - Massive amount, contributes to ==light pollution==[^8] in cinema
“19 percent made a phone call” - Unbelievable

**P3 Topic Sentence:** Author creates a clear distinction between using a phone while watching a TV and being at the cinema.

*Maddox contrasts electronic multitasking at home with its disruptive impact in cinemas to reinforce his disapproval of phone use during films*

“adds a layer of enjoyment” - Acknowledges the new hobby and its benefits
“a dismal and distracting development” - Underscores usage of devices at the cinema
“bring their chickens into church services” - Humorously adds …

*add more purpose*
