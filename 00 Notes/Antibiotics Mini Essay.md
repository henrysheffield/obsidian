# Antibiotics Mini Essay

## Essay

[[Antibiotics]] are essential tools in the medical world. They offer treatments for [[Bacteria]] and save lives, inversely though, they can also create new strands of deadly bacteria that cannot be treated if not used correctly. 

Antibiotics can be taken orally (through liquids or pills), topically (e.g. creams) or injections. They work by either killing the bacteria in a patient or slowing and suspending the growth of new bacteria cells. The first antibiotic, discovered in 1928 [^1] was [[Penicillin]], which was found on growing mold in a petri dish by accident. Scientists found that a type of fungus produced this substance. Nowadays, penicillin is still an effective antibiotic, sold in natural and semi-synthetic forms. 

However, a drawback of antibiotics is antibiotic resistance. This occurs when an antibiotic is misused, overused or another reason and the bacteria mutates to withstand a certain antibiotic. This produces deadly new strands of bacteria that cannot be treated straight away. For instance, it is estimated that antimicrobial resistance was responsible for up to 1.27 million global deaths in 2019[^2].  An example of a strand is MRSA (originating from [[Staphylococcus Aureus]]), a bacteria resistant to $\beta$-lactam antibiotics (e.g. pencillin) which can be difficult to treat within humans.

Ultimately, antibiotics are an amazing discovery in medicine, but should be used responsibly in order to save lives and prevent new resistant bacteria types from emerging.
