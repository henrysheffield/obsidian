# Antibiotics

## Notes

Antibiotics are a powerful, prescription-only treatment for [[Bacteria|bacterial infections]] and work in two ways:

1. Kill bacteria (bactericidal)
2. Prevent growth (bacteriostatic)

The word *antibiotic* means anything that will stop [[Bacteria|bacteria]] from growing.

One of the first discovered antibiotics was [[Penicillin|penicillin]] which is a type of fungus that was found in mould by accident.

However, the are ineffective against [[Virus|viral infections]] like the common flu or COVID-19.

Importantly, misuse / overuse of an antibiotic (e.g. a patient was given **the incorrect** antibiotic or **overused it** can cause [[Antibiotic Resistance]]) this is when bacteria develop immunity against an antibiotic due to [[Evolution]]. Creating deadly strands like MRSA (originating from [[Staphylococcus Aureus]]–a skin bacteria)
