# Antibody

## Notes

- Y Shaped [[Proteins|protein]]
- Produced by immune system
- Fixes a *specific* thing (example: one specific strand of a virus)
- Destroys foreign invadors
- Once created, your body produces it if that specific thing reappears (e.g same strand of [[Virus]]
	→This is what vaccines do, they aid your body in creating an antibody

**Foreign Antigens**
- Originate outside of body
- Non self
- COVID-19 example

**Autoantigens**
- Self
- Helathy
- Originate form your body
