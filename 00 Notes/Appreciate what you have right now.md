# Appreciate what you have right now

## Notes

I want you to take a short moment to appreciate all of the things that you have, and also consider if you want to change anything. At the moment, **you have access to good food, shelter, warmth, education, and a loving family**. Moreover, you are able to enjoy your passions and hobbies, such as reading, muay thai, cooking, french, and more. It must be realised that *not everyone has these opportunities*. Now, I would like to ask the question, would you change anything from your life?

In [[Meditations|Meditations]] Marcus Aurelius wrote, “Change. Nothing inherently bad in the process”[^1] proposing the idea that change is not a terrible thing, however, he adds a startling contradiction, “nothing inherently good in the result,” Aurelius hopes to convey to himself that why change can be good, the result may not differ too much to his life at the moment.[^2]

As Aurelius was battling the Germanic tribes, it would have been necessary to constantly change his strategies and tactics–supporting his idea of “nothing inherently bad in the process.” However, certain strategies (like the Roman human buffer tactic) were consistently shown to be powerful, thereby, making change less essential in the battlefield. Aurelius tries to appreciate the power of his army, and hopes to preserve some of its brilliant tactics, by accepting new change, but also being [[Consistent|consistent]] with most strategies.

Consider the following scenario, you like your [[Lenovo Finally Listened The Most ThinkPad ThinkPad Ever|Thinkpad]] but are not entirely happy with it, and you are thinking of buying a new laptop. According to Aurelius, the change may be good, but the result has a likelihood of being not massively different from your current life. Therefore, it has some benefit in changing, but it is incredibly more important to start acting. **Instead of buying a new gadget, you should continue to appreciate what you have, in the case of the Thinkpad, be grateful that you have a laptop.**

In conclusion, change is a good thing, but it is also immensely important to appreciate whatever items that you currently have. Today, take the time to point out to yourself everything that you feel good about, shelter, warmth, parents–understand that not everyone has access to this, so you should make the absolute most out of it.
