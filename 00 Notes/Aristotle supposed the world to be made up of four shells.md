# Aristotle supposed the world to be made up of four shells

## Notes

Aristotle believed  that the Earth was made up of **four concentric shells** and a **fifth, mysterious** element which he named *ether*.

1. **Earth:** The solid ball at the absolute centre. 
2. **Water:** The ocean layer surrounding the earth. 
3. **Air:** The atmosphere layer above the water. 
4. **Fire:** An invisible outer shell that occasionally becomes visible in flashes of lightning.

---

Beyond the sphere of fire lies the [[Cosmos]], which Aristotle believed makes up the universe. He named it **ether**.

* **Ether:** An unearthly, perfect fifth element that makes up the universe beyond the terrestrial shells.
* **Etymology:** From a Latin derivative, the name became **quintessence** (*quinta essentia* = "fifth element").
