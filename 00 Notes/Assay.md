# Assay

## Notes

Scientific test used to measure, analyse or quantify a specific genetic characteristic.

e.g. Analysing DNA sequences (ie. mutations)

Assays are tests used to measure concentration of drug in blood, presence of antibodies or other things*****

### myDNA

1. Assays brought in at around 150mL samples
2. **Aliquots**[^1] used to divide sample into many smaller samples
3. This way, you have many smaller samples to defrost **once** rather than defrosting and frosting a large sample constantly–this would denature the assay
