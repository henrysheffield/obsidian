**1. Atmosphere Fundamentals**

- **Role in Life:** Regulates temperature (greenhouse effect), protects from UV radiation (ozone layer), and provides essential gases (O₂ for respiration, CO₂ for photosynthesis).
- **Layers (Bottom to Top Properties):**
    - _Troposphere:_ Where weather happens; highest pressure/density; temperature decreases with altitude.
    - _Stratosphere:_ Contains the ozone layer; temperature increases with altitude.
    - _Mesosphere:_ Coldest layer; where meteors burn up; temperature decreases with altitude.
    - _Thermosphere:_ Very high temperatures; contains ionosphere (auroras); extremely low density.

**2. Isotopic Notation & Stability**

- **Notation:** ZA​X (where A = Mass Number [Protons + Neutrons], Z = Atomic Number [Protons], X = Element Symbol).
- **Nuclear Stability:** Driven by the balance between the strong nuclear force (attractive) and electrostatic repulsion (pushing protons apart).
- **NZ Graph Rules (Neutron to Proton ratio):**
    - _Elements Z < 20:_ Stable N:Z ratio is roughly 1:1.
    - _Elements Z > 20:_ Stable ratio increases (requires more neutrons to remain stable).
    - _Above the band of stability (too many neutrons):_ Undergoes Beta-minus decay.
    - _Below the band of stability (too many protons):_ Undergoes Beta-plus decay.
    - _Heavy elements (Z > 83):_ Undergoes Alpha decay.

**3. Nuclear Decay Equations**

- **Alpha (α) Decay:** Emits a helium nucleus (24​He).
    - _Equation Structure:_ ZA​X→Z−2A−4​Y+24​He
- **Beta Minus (β⁻) Decay:** A neutron turns into a proton, emitting an electron (−10​e).
    - _Equation Structure:_ ZA​X→Z+1A​Y+−10​e
- **Beta Plus (β⁺) Decay:** A proton turns into a neutron, emitting a positron (+10​e).
    - _Equation Structure:_ ZA​X→Z−1A​Y++10​e
- **Gamma (γ) Decay:** Emits a high-energy photon (00​γ), usually accompanying other decays. Leaves mass and atomic number unchanged.

**4. Half-Life Calculations**

- **Definition:** The time taken for half the radioactive nuclei in a sample to decay.
- **Formula:** Nt​=N0​×(0.5)n
    - Nt​ = Amount remaining
    - N0​ = Initial amount
    - n = Number of half-lives (Total time elapsed ÷ Half-life duration)
