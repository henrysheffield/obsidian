## Atmosphere

The atmosphere is the envelope of gases surrounding Earth that sustains life and regulates planetary temperature.

> [!TIP] **Summary**
> The atmosphere is a region of gases surrounding Earth. It is essential for life. It regulates temperature ([[Greenhouse Effect]]), protects from UV radiation ([[Ozone Layer]]) and provides essential gases like $O_2$ for respiration or $CO_2$ for photosynthesis.

---

### Composition

The atmosphere consists of a specific mixture of gases. While nitrogen and oxygen are the primary components, trace gases play a significant role in the greenhouse effect and climate regulation.

| **Gas**                                    | **Percentage** |
| ------------------------------------------ | -------------- |
| **Nitrogen ($N_{2}$)**                     | 78.1%          |
| **Oxygen ($O_{2}$)**                       | 20.9%          |
| **Argon ($Ar$)**                           | 0.93%          |
| **Trace Gases** ($CO_{2}$, $CH_{4}$, etc.) | ~0.07%         |

---

### Atmospheric Layers

The atmosphere is divided into distinct layers based on temperature fluctuations and physical characteristics.

| **Layer**        | **Distance (km)** | **Key Characteristics**                                                                                        |
| ---------------- | ----------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Troposphere**  | 0–12              | Surface layer; contains most oxygen; where **weather** occurs. ‘Troposphere’ = ‘sphere of change’ in greek     |
| **Stratosphere** | 12–50             | Contains the **Ozone Layer** (UV absorption); flight path for jets.                                            |
| **Mesosphere**   | 50–80             | The "middle atmosphere"; coldest layer (down to -90°C); **meteors** burn up here.                              |
| **Thermosphere** | 80–700            | Contains the **Kármán Line**; temperatures reach 2000°C (though heat transfer is low due to particle density). |
| **Exosphere**    | 700–10,000        | The transition into outer space; molecular density is extremely low.                                           |
