- A cell is the basic building block of all living things

3 Core properties for all cells
1. Cell membrane (separation of cell and outside environment)
2. Cytoplasm (Jelly like fluid which organelles or molecules can flow through inside the cell)
3. [[DNA]] (Genetic material of cell)

**2 types of cells**

[[Eukaryotic]]
- Contains **membrane-closed** organelles
- More advanced (eg. plants, animals)

[[Prokaryotic]]
- No nucleus (but still has genetic material)
- Unicellular organisms only (e.g. bacteria)

[[Nucleolus]] - where ribosomes made
[[Ribosome]] - Synthesises [[Proteins]]
[[Endoplasmic Reticulum (ER)]] - Some ribosomes attach, can be made rough (with ribosomes) or smooth (without)

Proteins emerge from [[Endoplasmic Reticulum (ER)]] in small vesicles, the [[Golgi Apparatus]] receives them

the [[Golgi Apparatus]] folds porteins in other shapes OR adds molecules like lipids to create different usable functions for the proteins

Vacoule - sac-like structures which stores different materials

[[Lysosome]] - garbage collections for dead cells, contains [[Enzyme|enzymes]] which break up cellular debris

[[Mitochondrian]] - energy powerhouse for cells, makes ATP molecules for energy

[[Cytoskeleton]] - shape

[[Chloroplast]] - wehre photosynthesis occurs; green because of chlorophyll

Flagella- tail to propel a cell (sperm, bacteria)

By packaging the DNA into chromosomes, the cell is able to reproduce either two identical daughter cells (Mitosis) with the exact same genetic information. Or, produce 4 genetically different daughter cells (Meiosis).
