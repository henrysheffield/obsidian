# Biology 

## Areas

[[Genetics]] - Study of genes, heredity, and genetic variation in organisms
[[Microbiology]] - Study of microscopic organisms, especially bacteria

## Cell Biology

[[Amino Acids]] - Building blocks of proteins; 20 types connected by peptide bonds
[[ATP]] - Primary energy carrier molecule used by all living cells
[[Cholesterol]] - Steroid lipid that buffers membrane fluidity in animal cells
[[Eukaryotic]] - Complex cells with membrane-bound organelles and a defined nucleus
[[Fluid Mosaic Model]] - Accepted model describing the dynamic structure of cell membranes
[[Mitochondria]] - Powerhouse organelle that generates ATP for the cell
[[Mitochondrion]] - Singular; organelle that produces ATP via cellular respiration
[[Organelle]] - Specialised structures inside a cell that carry out specific tasks
[[Osmosis]] - Passive net movement of water across a selectively permeable membrane
[[Peptioglycan]] - Rigid mesh-like polymer forming the cell wall of most bacteria
[[Phospholipid]] - Amphipathic molecule that forms the semi-permeable cell membrane bilayer
[[Prokaryotic]] - Simple cells lacking a defined nucleus and membrane-bound organelles
[[Proteins]] - Chains of amino acids that carry out essential cellular functions

## Genetics

[[Alleles]] - Different versions of a gene (e.g. dominant B or recessive b)
[[DNA]] - Deoxyribonucleic acid; hereditary material containing the genetic code
[[DNA and Cellular Division - How are they related?]] - Essay on DNA replication during cell division
[[Gametes]] - Haploid sex cells (sperm and eggs in humans)
[[Gene]] - Functional unit of heredity made of DNA that codes for proteins
[[Genes]] - Basic physical unit of heredity; sections of DNA with genetic instructions
[[Genotype & Phenotype]] - Genetic makeup versus observable characteristics
[[Hetero]] - Prefix meaning "different" (e.g. heterozygous Bb)
[[Homo]] - Prefix meaning "same" (e.g. homozygous BB)
[[How are proteins made from DNA?]] - Essay on transcription and translation processes
[[Mendel's Bean Experiment]] - Foundational genetics experiments using pea plants
[[Punnett Squares]] - Visual grid to predict genetic outcomes of crosses
[[RNA]] - Single-stranded nucleic acid essential for protein synthesis
[[RNA vs DNA]] - Comparison of structure and function between the two nucleic acids
[[revised genetics guide]] - Roadmap for studying genetics from the ground up
[[The Structure of DNA]] - Video notes on DNA's double helix molecular structure

## Microbiology

[[Antibiotics]] - Treatments that kill bacteria or prevent their growth
[[Bacteria]] - Ubiquitous prokaryotic organisms a few micrometres in length
[[Gram Negative Bacteria]] - Bacteria with thin peptioglycan wall that stains pink/red
[[Gram Positive Bacteria]] - Bacteria with thick peptioglycan wall that stains purple
[[Gram Staining]] - Technique to differentiate bacteria by cell wall composition
[[Meiosis]] - Cell division producing four genetically different gamete cells
[[Mitosis]] - Somatic cell division producing two identical daughter cells (PMAT)
[[Staphylococcus Aureus]] - Gram positive bacterium commonly found on skin and nose

## Body Systems

[[Antibody]] - Y-shaped protein produced by the immune system to neutralise pathogens
[[Antigen]] - Foreign substance that triggers an immune response
[[Hormones]] - Chemical messengers regulating growth, metabolism and mood
[[Mandible]] - Largest, strongest and only movable bone in the human skull
[[Orthodontics]] - Branch of dentistry treating dental and facial irregularities
