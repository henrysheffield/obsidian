# I found the best way to take book notes

# Book Note-Taking Framework

> [!abstract] Core Rule
> 
> **Automation is the enemy of retention.** The deliberate friction of using a pencil and manually typing notes is what makes them stick.

## The 3-Phase Framework

### Phase 1: Mindful Annotation

- **Goal:** Minimal, high-signal curation for your future self. Leave breathing room for a re-read.
    
- **Action:** Never underline massive blocks of text. Use a pencil and sticky tabs.
    
- **The Key:**
    
    - `[ Brackets ]` = Long, important passages (saves page space).
        
    - `◯ Circle` = Jargon, keywords, or vocabulary to define later.
        
    - `☆ Star` = Absolute core insights.
        
    - `? Question` = Confusing points requiring clarity/research.
        

### Phase 2: Manual Exporting & Sorting

- **Goal:** Extract annotations into Obsidian. **Do not automate this.**
- Link: [[Ryan Holiday's 3-Step System for Reading Like a Pro]] uses similar method
    
- ==**Card Anatomy:** Every extracted note must have:==
    
    1. A short quote + page number.
        
    2. Your own 1-sentence analysis.
        
    3. Contextual sources cited by the author to look up later.
        
- **Sorting:** Don't let notes sit in a random stack. Manually group your notes by **emerging themes** to map out the argument's structure.
    

### Phase 3: Active Reflection

- **Goal:** Force passive knowledge into active use so you don't forget it.
    
- **Strategy A (The Lesson Plan):** Imagine teaching the topic to a smart peer who knows slightly less than you. Map the thesis, terms, and how you'd handle their objections.
    
- **Strategy B (Letter to a Dead Author):** Write a mock letter to the author. Praise their strong points, but aggressively critique their blind spots and imagine their defense.
    

## Daily Reading Routine

|**Frequency**|**Focus**|**Action**|
|---|---|---|
|**Daily**|**Read & Tag**|Read with pencil in hand. Use your symbols and drop a sticky tab on marked pages.|
|**Weekly**|**The Export**|30 mins on weekends. Flip _only_ to tabs. Manually type quotes into Obsidian, define circled words, and add your 1-sentence analysis.|
|**Monthly**|**Reflect**|1 hour at the end of a book. Pick one challenging concept and spend 20 minutes drafting a fake lesson plan or essay about it.|
