# Boredom makes life meaningful

![Boredom makes life meaningful](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0D0Qe-N4ZAM)

## Notes

Boredom is not one emotion, but it is multiple
	→Example: Feeling lazy, tired and ‘unproductive’
	
Boredom is actually essential for creative thinking and experiencing new things.

**How?**

Say your bored at an office job, that is completely natural. And then you decide to get a job as a skydiver (idk). Here, boredom encouraged you to try new experiences and be a different person.

$$Boredom\rightarrow Contemplation \rightarrow Meaning$$


>[!QUOTE]
>”All truly great thoughts are conceived while walking.”
>Nietzsche - [6:24]

This is completely true, walking is simple and allows you to think for yourself in peace.

### Author’s guide on how to embrace boredom

- You cannot embrace completely straight away (e.g. Do you think that you will stop using your phone?)
- Progress in small ‘boredom chores’
- Examples: looking out bus window instead of being on phone, drinking coffee while doing nothing but relaxing

### Disagreement

Author stated that often he will just eat a meal without reading, watching a video as a way to embrace boredom.

I believe that you can use that empty time not to enjoy your food but to do tasks like:
- Nutrition tracking
- Reading (productive to me)
- Watching a video in french (helps practice language)
## Application

- [ ] Usually you listen to music while you walk, occasionally just embrace the boredom of simply walking without any music. Instead, listen to the birds chirping or the wind noises–this can be a great time for random thoughts.
