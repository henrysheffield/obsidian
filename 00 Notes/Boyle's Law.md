# Boyle's Law

## Notes

**Gas is dictated by:**
- Pressure
- Temperature
- Volume
- Amount (in [[Mole]])

- Formulated by Robert Boyle in 1662
- ==Volume is inversely proportional to Pressure== e.g. if volume increases, pressure decreases and vice-versa.

$$PV=Constant$$
$$P_{1}V_{1}=P_{2}V_2$$
