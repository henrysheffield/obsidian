# Budj Bim

## Notes

A UNESCO World-[[Heritage|heritage]] listed site[^1]. It is home to **one of the world’s oldest aquaculture system, dating back to ==6,600 years**.==

Built by the ==Gunditjmara== people who built physical houses, dismissing the misconception that aboriginal people were entirely nomadic.

This community engineered an aquaculture system for ==hunting eels (*Anguilla australis)*==

Provided a year-round food source==(**societal impact**)==
