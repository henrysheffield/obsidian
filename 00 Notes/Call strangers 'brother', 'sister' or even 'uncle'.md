# Call strangers 'brother', 'sister' or even 'uncle'

>”For instance, he [Hierocles] advised his students to refer to strangers as “brother” or “sister” … as a constant reminder that we should treat other people as if they really are our relatives, as reason counsels that we are all in the same boat together.”

Hierocles says that we should “refer to strangers as ‘brother’ or ‘sister’” as a means to show proper respect and [[Kindness]] to everybody in the world. In practice, this is a bit weird to employ in a literal sense. However, we can definitely be nicer to every human in the world right? We are “all in the same boat togther” demonstrating that we are all humans capable of being kind.
