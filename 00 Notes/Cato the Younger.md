# Note

## Notes

**Cato the Younger**
- Roman General
- Studied Stoicism, immediately began to live more modestly and [[[Minimalism|minimalist]]
- Civil war against Julius Caesar
- When defeat was inevitable he committed suicide 

Suicide:
- Tried to kill himself by cutting his throat
- Failed, doctors tried to save him
- He stopped them and ripped his throat out.

Demonstration of [[Integrity]] to the extreme.
