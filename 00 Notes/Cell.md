# Cell

## Notes

Cells are the basic building blocks of all living things. They are divided into [[Eukaryotic]] cells (complex, membrane bound organelles, e.g. plants, animals) and [[Prokaryotic]] cells (simple, e.g. bacteria)

**3 Properties for ALL cells**

1. Cell membrane (separation of the cell and the outside environment)
2. Cytoplasm (jelly like fluid where nutrients and other molecules can flow)
3. DNA (Genetic material, instructions for how a cell functions and survives)
