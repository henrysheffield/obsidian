# Change, nothing bad nor good

>”Change: nothing inherently bad in the process, nothing inherently good in the result.”

Appreciate what you have (e.g. your thinkpad). No need for uneccessary change to a *new laptop* if this one gets the job done fine.

Maybe it could be better for productivity, but not by a grand amount →don’t change.
