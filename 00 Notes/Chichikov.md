# Chichikov

## Notes

An unremarkable looking man, originally described as a bit ‘plump’ but a good fellow who is semi-kind. He is neither the hero nor the villan, but he is the **protagonist**, being both semi-good and semi-bad simultaneously.

His main quality is to venture to different landowners and propose a strange deal–he wishes to buy dead serfs who have not been filed as dead yet by the census. This saves the landowners taxes but is rather bizarre.

He is likely a con-man or simply a bit odd.
