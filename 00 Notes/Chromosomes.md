# Chromosomes

## Notes

*Chromosomes* contain a long strand of [[DNA]] which are tightly packaged around [[Proteins|proteins]] called *histones*. 

Chromosomes are located within the [[Eukaryotic|eukaryotic cell’s]] nucleus.

Chromosomes are only visible when the cell undergoes replication under a microscope.

The centromere is the ball in the middle of the chromosome (see below diagram)
