# Climate Change

## Notes

Long-term, significant shift of temperature in the earth, where the surface warms (*global warming*). Primarily driven by the [[Greenhouse Effect|greenhouse gases]] like $CO_2$ which are released by human activity (e.g. burning fossil fuels).
