# Collision Theory 

## Notes

For a collision to result in a chemical reaction, the following must be satisfied:

1. **Physical collision** - Particles must actually hit each other
2. **Correct Orientation** - Particles must hit each other at the correct angle
3. **Activation Energy** - Particles must collide with sufficient kinetic energy

![[Collision Theory-1777183653717.webp]]

## Factors that affect reaction rate

1. **Concentration / Pressure** - More particles → more chance of collisions
2. **Temperature** - Heat makes particles move faster
3. **Surface area** - Breaking a solid into smaller pieces exposes more surface area
4. **Catalysts** - Provide alternate route for reaction, making it easier to begin
