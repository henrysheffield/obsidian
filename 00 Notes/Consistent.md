# Consistent

## Notes

`adjective`
`/kənˈsɪs.tənt/`

Something that remains steady, unchanging or harmonious over time.

1. Always behaving or happening in a similar way
	→*Consistent economic growth*
2. Logical (ideas that agree with each other)

## Examples / Problems

*To achieve any goal, whether it be fitness, studying, languages or friendships. The upmost important thing is to be **consistent***.

To achieve the splits, you must be consistent a minimum of 5 times per week, even if it is just for 5 minutes.
