# Core idea of stoicism

> “Stoicism is not about suppressing or hiding emotion - rather, it is about acknowledging our emotions, reflecting on what causes them and redirecting them for our own good.” 
> 2

This is the core idea of [[Stoicism]]. It is not just about being austere and emotionless, it is about reflecting on who we are, where our pain lies and how to “redirect them for our own good.”
