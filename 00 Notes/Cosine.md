# Cosine

## Notes

Horizontal factor in the [[Unit Circle]]  $(\cos \theta, \sin \theta)$
$$domain \in \mathbb{R}, range \in [-1,1]$$
Even symmetric function ($\cos (- \theta)=\cos \theta$)

It is just [[Sine|sin]] shifted by 90 degrees $$\cos (\theta)=\sin(\theta + 90^{\circ})=\sin (\theta + \frac{\pi}{2})$$
