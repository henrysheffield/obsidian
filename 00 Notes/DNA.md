r# DNA

## Notes

DNA (*Deoxyribonucleic acid or Acide désoxyribonucléique*) is the [[Heredity]] material in organisms. It creates a code for how your body should function and appear, with almost every one of your cells having the *exact same DNA*.

The information is coded in 4 bases:
1. Adenine (A)
2. Guanine (G)
3. Cytosine (C)
4. Thymine (T)

Which form base pairs (ideally AT and CG or TA and GC) AND attach to a sugar and phosphate molecule to form a [[Nucleotide]] (a phosphate group + sugar + nitrogenous base)

DNA is typically stored in a [[Cell|cell’s]] nucleus (*called nuclear DNA*) but a small amount can also be found in the [[Mitochondria|mitochondria]] (*called mitrochondrial DNA or mtDNA*)

Importantly, DNA has the ability to replicate (e.g. the exact same DNA when a cell breeds)
