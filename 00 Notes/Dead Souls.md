# Dead Souls

![[Dead Souls-1781779808397.webp]]

## Notes

Russian classic by [[Nikolay Gogol]]
*Translated by ROBERT A. MAGUIRE*

## Chapter Summaries

**Chapter 1**
- Introduces characters like Chichikov
- Plays card games
- Initially meets core characters like: **Nzdryov, Manilov, Police chief, etc**

**Chapter 2**
- Chichikov meets Manilov - a kind man who is so kind that he is described as “full of sugar”
- Manilov welcomes him into his estate
- Introduces Chichikov to his wife–they are absurdly in love
- Everyone is super happy
- 2 kids: Themistoclus and Anticides
- Later, Chichikov makes his first strage request–he wishes to buy dead serfs who have not been filed yet
- Manilov is initially a bit bewildered and frightened but agrees
- Chichikov leaves

**Chapter 3**
- Focus on Kobushka, an old widow who Chichikov calls “a hag”
- Kobushka has a moderate amount of serfs, knows all of their names by heart
- Welcomes Chichikov and encourages him to buy produce
- Chichikov agrees to buy produce but in the future
- Rather, he insists on acquiring 18 dead serfs
- Kobushka is extremely hesistant at first–which angers Chichikov
- Eventually he convinces her and also give 15 roubles + a stamp
- Eats a beautiful meal prepared by the widow’s daughter
- Leaves in a britka hastily, promising to return

**Chapter 4**
- Chichikov plans to go to Sobakevich but is diverted by Nozdryov
- Nozdryov is ardent card player, but cheats often
- He is **emotionally labile** due to an extremely volatile mood 
	- He will often be the most lovely friend, offering expensive wine but a minute later threaten to kill someone
- Originally he welcomes Chichikov with lots of wine and food to his estate–everybody is very happy
- When Chichikov makes his strange request, Nozdryov initially demands for a trade e.g. buying uneccessary objects first
- When Chichikov declines, an argument pursues
- Nozdryov demands Chichikov to play cards to settle the deal but to Chichikov, it seems highly suspicious
- Everyone goes to bed
- Chichikov wakes up early and goes outside to the garden, sees Nozdryov
- In morning Nozdryov is more relaxed but then the same argument happens
- They agree to play checkers
- Nozdryov is caught cheating and Chichikov refuses to play
- The argument is extremely heated and Nozdryov orders his servants to beat Chichikov–who is weak and can do nothing–but just at that same moment the Police Chief walks in and arrests Nozdryov for a past offence
- Chichikov quickly runs away to his britka with Selfrian* and leaves
