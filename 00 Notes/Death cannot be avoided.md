# Death cannot be avoided

> “Death is necessary and cannot be avoided. I mean, where am I going to go to get away from it?”
> Epictetus - pp.6

Clear morality theme, death “cannot be avoided,” rendering it useless to try to combat the natural phenomenon of death. Therefore, [[Should you be afraid of death?|you should not be afraid of death.]]
