# Democritus

## Notes

- Did not create democracy, the term comes from *demokratia* and originated before he was born
- Proposed first atomic theory: all matter in universe is made up of tiny, indivisible things called *atoms*
- He also found geometric principles e.g. cone is $\frac{1}{3}$ size of cylinder
