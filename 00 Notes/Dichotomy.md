# Dichotomy

A divison or contrast between two things that are completely opposite, [[Mutually Exclusive|mutually exclusive]], or entirely different. 

## Notes

## Philosophy

A fundamental [[Stoicism|stoicism]] principle which divides the earth into two categories. 

### 1. Things that are within our control.

- Our thoughts
- Beliefs
- How we react to situations

### 2. Things that are outside our control

- Outcomes
- The weather
- Opinions of others
- Height

By understanding what we can and cannot control, we should focus on what we *can control* and not get worked up or cry over what *we cannot control*.

## [[Biology|Biology]]

You can compare living and non-living things as a dichotomy. 

Abiotic (non-living) and biotic (living) are complete opposites.


## Examples

Instead of focusing on something like **getting a promotion** we should instead focus on **our preparation, our attitude, and our effort**. If you don’t get the promotion, do not get sad about this, it is **out of your control**.

Instead of getting annoyed that you are not the tallest person in Australia and feeling envious of tall people, realise that you have almost no control over your height. You can however, control the food you eat, the nutrients you get to encourage height growth, but never the final result.
