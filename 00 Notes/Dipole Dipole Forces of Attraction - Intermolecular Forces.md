## Captured Notes

- Dipole-dipole forces are intermolecular forces between polar molecules

## Carbon Monoxide ($CO$)

- C has an electronegativity of 2.5 while O has an electronegativity of 3.5
- This causes a $\Delta EN = 1.0$ 
- The molecule is **non-polar** due to the geometry, cancelling out the charges 

Why is Carbon Monoxide non polar despite a difference in electronegativity? :: The linear geometry cancels out the polarity, causing it to be non polar.

## Carbon Monoxide ($CO$) vs Sulphur Dioxide

- Both have the same $\Delta EN = 1.0$ 
- Suplur Dioxide is bent
- Carbon Monoxide is linear
