# Discipline of action

>”the discipline of action tells us how to behave in the world.”
>pp.25

My opinion of how to behave in this world:

### Virtue of being [[Kindness|kind]], respectful, understanding and sympathetic

e.g. making mum a cup of tea, performing [[Active Listening]] to everyone you meet rather than just talking about yourself, doing the laundry, cooking breakfast

it is the small kind things that holds you to your virtues properly.
