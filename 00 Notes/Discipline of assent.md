# Discipline of assent

>”discipline of assent tells us how to react to situations … what is reasonable and not reasonable to think.”
>25

You must react stoically (that is, indifferent to pain and pleasure) in the majority of situations (especially painfully)

### Example

The night prior, you pack a lunch for yourself at school tomorrow, but somebody decides to eat the lunch the next morning. Instead, of being angry and buying an untasty wrap from coles and letting it ruin your day, you must release that it is in the *past, and therefore out of your control*

Therefore, you should not react negatively and become uneccessarily saddened.
