# Discipline of desire

>”The discipline of desire tells us what is and is not proper to want.”
>pp.24

What is proper to want:

- Food
- Shelter
- Family
- Education (this is a gift)

What is *not* proper to want

- New thinkpad when you thinkpad works just fine
- New clothes when your clothes suit just fine
- Angry at others to make yourself feel better (this is a lie, it is false, it will only aggravate the situation)
