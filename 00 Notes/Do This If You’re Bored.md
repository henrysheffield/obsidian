# Do This If You’re Bored

![Do This If You’re Bored](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ATKZaZ1ZXcs)

## Notes

### 1. Clean your room
- Set a timer (e.g. 10-15 minutes) and try to clean your room as fast as you can
- It is like a game–but productive
### 2. Deck of cards

- Get a deck of cards
- Whatever number you get do the corresponding number of pushups
- Continue until you cannot do anymore OR deck is empty

### 3. Sell items 

- Look around your room
- What can you sell of facebook marketplace for some cash?
- Thinkpads, monitor, cabinet, tons of little things, etc

### 4. Wall / Window scroll (i added)

- If bored, just look at your wall or window
- It isn’t very entertaining but it is a good pass time.
