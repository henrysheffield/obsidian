# Elution wash out DNA with beads

## Notes

DNA is collected from the patient using their cheek cells and a swab, we must separate these cells away from the DNA.

1. Sample
2. Beads bind to DNA
3. Using a magnet, the beads and DNA go to one side of the test tube
4. Wash out the mixture
5. Perform [[Elution]] to separate the beads and the DNA
6. Now, you have clean DNA
