# Epictetus

## Notes

A Greek [[Stoicism|stoic]] philosopher.

Epictetus was not his real name, which is lost to us. The name means “acquired,'“ reflecting the fact that he was a slave.^[1]

After Nero’s death in $68~CE$, Epictetus was freed by his master (a common practice in Rome for intelligent/educated slaves)
