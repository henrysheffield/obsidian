## Notes

Before the end-of-year exams (Early November) I should be able to write **high quality** English essays **quickly**

**Numbers:**

- A **900 - 1,200 word** essay should be easy for me in 60 minutes
- I should write at about **20 - 25 wpm**

## How to achieve?

I must write a minimum of 3 mini-essays per week and a minimum of 1 long essay per week.

> [!TIP]
> Week starts on Friday afternoon so you can quickly get a long essay done.
> Try to finish all long and mini essays before Monday

The essays can be on any concept, book or idea that you would like to talk about.

They will all remain on obsidian, but **long essays must always be first written on paper**

- The one long essay per week **must be done on pen and paper** you must train your hand muscles to write 1000 words
- Dedicate 50% of the long essays to a fiction classical book, this is exactly what you will be doing for English
- 20 - 30 % of mini essays can be on fiction but this is less important
