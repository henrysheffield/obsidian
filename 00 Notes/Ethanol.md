## Ethanol

**Ethanol** (ethyl alcohol) is a simple **alkanol** that serves as a versatile chemical in industrial, medical, and consumer applications. It is widely recognized as a solvent, a potent antiseptic, and a fuel source.

### Structure

![[Ethanol-1777024659020.webp]]

- **Molecular Formula:** $C_2H_5OH$
    
- **Functional Group:** Contains one **hydroxyl group** ($-OH$) attached to a two-carbon ethyl chain.
    
- **Geometry:** The oxygen atom has a bent geometry, contributing to the molecule's overall polarity.
    

### Physical and Chemical Properties

- **Appearance:** Colorless, volatile, and highly flammable liquid.
    
- **Intermolecular Forces:** Exhibits **strong hydrogen bonding** due to the presence of the hydroxyl group.
    
- **Solubility:** Completely **miscible** with water. The small hydrocarbon chain is not large enough to override the polarity of the $-OH$ group.
    
- **Thermal Constants:** * **Boiling Point:** 78.2°C
    
    - **Melting Point:** -114.1°C
        
- **Biological Interaction:** Its small size and polar nature allow it to move through cell membranes and cross the **blood-brain barrier** with ease.
    

---

### Comparison with Alkanes

Because of its ability to form hydrogen bonds, ethanol has a much higher boiling point than **ethane** ($C_2H_6$), which only experiences weak London dispersion forces.
