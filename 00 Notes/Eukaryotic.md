# Eukaryotic

## Notes

A type of cell that contains:

1. Membrane bound [[Organelle|organelles]]
2. Clearly defined nucleus (area where [[DNA]] lives) [^1]

Generally is the type of cell which inhibits animals or plants, usually more complex
