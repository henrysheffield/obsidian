# Every hour is meaningful

> “Every hour of the day give vigorous attention … to the performance of the task in hand with precise analysis, with unaffected dignity, with human sympathy, with dispassionate justice … as it were the last of your life.”

Powerful statement. This demonstrates the importance of every task, action and why they should all be meaningful and carried out to fullest extent.

→Treat every task (e.g. math exam) as it were the last of your life. You would want to do amazing, so why not do it now?
