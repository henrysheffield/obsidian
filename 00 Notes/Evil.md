# Note

## Notes

According to me, evil does not exist. Instead, it is **ignorance** which causes so-called ‘evil’. By not considering what other people think of your doing, you may be causing pain, devastation or terrible acts without realising it.

> [!QUOTE]
> “evil is often the result of lack of thought, meaning that people usually don’t *want* to do evil, and certainly don’t think of themselves as evildoers.”
> [[How To Be A Stoic]] - pp.112

Example (provided in [[How To Be A Stoic]])

Adolf Eichmann–architect of the Holocaust–believed he was doing a good job to society and was proud of his efficiency. However, he was ignorant to the fact of the millions of Jews who were murdered, starved or tortured.
