# Exponential Functions

## Notes

- Tend to have domain $\mathbb{R}$ but a restricted range
- Asymptotes


- Domain is $\mathbb{R}$
- Range is $(0, \infty)$ or  $\mathbb{R}^+$
