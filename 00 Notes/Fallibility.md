# Fallibility

## Notes

- Tendency to make mistakes, be wrong or fail to work as intended.
- Fundamental characteristic of human nature

## Examples

- Human fallibility - e.g. in the mind, mistakes
- System fallibility - e.g. the legal system can give false results / make mistakes (particularly present in [[Twelve Angry Men Overview]]
