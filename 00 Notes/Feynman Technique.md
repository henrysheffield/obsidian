# Feynman Technique

## Notes

A four step technique that improves comprehension and memory of a subject.
### 1. Choose concept

- Get a blank piece of paper, tablet or sound recording
- Choose your concept, it can be anything
### 2. Explain to a person who has absolutely no knowledge in the subject

- No fancy words / jargon
- You are not allowed to say ‘things’
- Explain concepts in your own words deeply
- *DNA is made of a long chain of nucleotides →DNA is an extremely long thread of string that is made of tiny parts which contain 3 objects: a sugar, a phosphate group and a base (the opposite of an acid) that can be: adenine, thymine, cytosine or guanine.*

### 3. Identify gaps in your knowledge

- Heavily scrutinise what you have written
- Either: ask yourself if someone else can understand what you have described or literally ask someone else if they can comprehend the idea according to your writing

### 4. Refine and simplify your knowledge

- Go back to the topic and comprehend the ideas in more detail
- Keep going in this loop until you fully understand the notion
