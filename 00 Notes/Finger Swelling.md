# Finger Swelling

## Notes

Problem: Swelling and itchiness in right hand during exercise in winter

- When you exercise: body pumps blood to working muscles
- Causes internal temperature to increase
- Directs blood to skin
- May cause blood vessels in fingers to widen →swelling (temporary)

From what I have researched, it does not seem to bad.
