# Foliation

## Notes

- Repetitive leaf-like layering noticeably present in some [[Metamorphic Rocks]]
- Gives it a pattern to see on the rock
- Forms from **differential stress** which is unequal pressure from multiple directions, causing a ‘smear’ appearance
- From latin word *folium* meaning *leaf*

![[Foliation-1780106944199.webp]]

## Difference of Foliation and Strata?

- Strata is present in [[Sedimentary Rocks]] caused by settling and and deposition of sediments over time
- Foliation occurs in [[Metamorphic Rocks]] which undergo immense heat and pressure
