## Summary

> [!TIP]
> Gamma radiation is a high frequency, low wavelength photon that travels at the speed of light, with zero charge, zero mass. Does not emit a particle like other types of radiation but still can be ionizing.
## Description

- Unlike alpha or beta radiation, gamma radiation is not a particle, it is an electromagnetic wave (or a *photon*)
- 0 charge
- 0 mass
- low ionizing power (due to low charge)
- very high penetrating power

Gamma radiation is unlike [[Alpha Radiation]] or [[Beta Radiation]] where a particle is emitted. Instead, gamma radiation is a high energy electromagnetic wave (or a *photon*)

### Properties

- Charge = 0
- Mass = 0
- High [[Frequency]]
- Short [[Wavelengths]]
- Travel in straight lines at speed of light 
- Extremely high penetrating power
- Low ionization but if they hit an atom, they strip the electron away.
