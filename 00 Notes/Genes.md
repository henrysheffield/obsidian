# Genes

## Notes

The basic physical and functional unit of [[Heredity]][^1]. 
Genes are sections of [[DNA]] (typically 10,000 - 50,000 base pairs long in a human body)
Typically, people have 2 copies of genes *each* gene, inherited from 2 parents.

Some genes act as **instructions** to make [[Proteins|proteins]], which are essential for the body to function. However, many genes **do not code for proteins**. 

> [!TIP]
> Most genes are the same in people, but a small amount (less than 1%) contain small differences in the DNA makeup. The small differences in the sequence of [[DNA]] bases are called [[Alleles]]
