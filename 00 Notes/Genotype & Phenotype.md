# Genotype & Phenotype

## Notes

The genotype is the specific combination of gene variants ([[Alleles]]) in specific combinations (e.g. $BB$, $Bb$)

The phenotype is the actual observable characteristic (e.g. brown hair, red eyes, etc)

The environment can influence a genotype.

## Example

Flamingos can appear pink, but this *phenotype* is determined by the food and *environment* they are in. This dictates whether the flamingo appears pink or white. [^1]
