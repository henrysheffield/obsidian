## Description

Gram Negative Bacteria is characterised by having a thinner cell wall made of less peptioglycan compared to [[Gram Positive Bacteria]]. Under [[Gram Staining]] it generally appears red or pink.
## Key Properties

- High resistance to antibiotics
- Presence of endotoxins
- Examples: *E.Coli, Salmonella*

### Flashcards

Why are Gram-negative bacteria generally more resistant to certain antibiotics like Penicillin? :: The outer membrane acts as a barrier that prevents the antibiotic from reaching the peptidoglycan layer.
