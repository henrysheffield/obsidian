> [!TIP] **Summary**
> Technique used to differentiate Gram positive (purple) and gram negative bacteria based on their colours due to their cell wall composition.

## Description

Gram Staining is a rapid, fundamental microbiology technique to distinguish between 2 types of bacteria: [[Gram Positive Bacteria]] (which appear purple) and [[Gram Negative Bacteria]] (pink/red). The technique yields a different colour of the bacteria based on the cell wall composition

This tool helps identify infection causes, aiding in selection of [[Antibiotics]]
## Key Ideas

Identifies type of bacteria based on their colour (due to their cell wall composition)

### Procedure

1. **Primary Stain (Crystal Violet):** Colours all cells purple.
2. **Mordant (Iodine):** Forms a complex with crystal violet, locking it into the cell wall.
3. **Decolorizer (Alcohol or Acetone):** Removes the dye from gram-negative cells but not gram-positive cells.
4. **Counterstain (Safranin):** Stains the colorless gram-negative cells pink/red.
