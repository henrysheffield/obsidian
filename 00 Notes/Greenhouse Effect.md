# Greenhouse Effect

## Notes

The Greenhouse Effect occurs when some solar radiation (heat energy) is trapped in the [[Atmosphere|atmosphere]] due to greenhouse gases absorbing the energy. This causes the atmosphere **to warm up**, accelerating [[Climate Change|climate change]].

Greenhouse gas examples: Carbon Dioxide ($CO_2$), Methane ($CH_{4}$) or Nitrous Oxide ($N_{2}O$)

> [!QUOTE]
> In 2024, the earth was $1.55^{\circ} C$ hotter than pre-industrial levels [^1]


![[Greenhouse Effect-1780800302416.webp]]
*Mechanisms responsible for the greenhouse effect.* [^2]

Key statement from the image: **”some of the infrared energy is absorbed and re-radiated in all directions … warming the Earth and lower atmosphere.”**

## Examples / Problems

Burning fossil fuels releases more Carbon Dioxide into the atmosphere, intensifying the effects of the Greenhouse Effect.
