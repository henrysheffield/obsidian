# Half Life

## Notes

Half life is a measure of how long a [[Radioisotope|radioactive nuclide]] takes to [[Radioactive Decay|decay]]. 

Two main formulas:

## 1. Base-2 formula (intuitive - easy)

$$N(t)=N_{0}(0.5)^{\frac{t}{t_{1/2}}}$$
*where*
$N(t)$ - Quantity remaining
$N_0$ - Initial quantity
$t$ - elapsed time
$t_{1/2}$ - half life of substance

This formula is used in most basic science class. Works for half lives only.

## 2. Base-e formula (calculus, more ‘correct’ approach for all cases)

$$N(t)=N_{0}~e^{- \lambda t}$$
*where lambda - radioactive decay constant*
$$\lambda = \frac{\ln(2)}{t_{1/2}}$$
*where*

$N(t)$ - Quantity remaining
$N_0$ - Initial quantity
$t$ - elapsed time
$t_{1/2}$ - half life of substance
$e$ - [[Euler’s number (e)]]
