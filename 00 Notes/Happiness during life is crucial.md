# Happiness during life is crucial

> “an olive might fall when ripe, blessing the earth which bore it and grateful to the tree which gave it growth.”
> 4.48

Life → death - happiness the entire way is absolutely imperative. The olive is grateful to be living from the tree, which allowed it to live.

You must also be grateful for the people you have grew up with. Aurelius uses an analogy of an olive, who is “grateful to the tree” because the tree has raised the olive from a seedling. Apply this into your life, be considerate and [[Kindness|kind]] to your [[Kind to relations (family, friends, etc)|parents, relatives, grand parents, friends and everyone]] who has shaped you, and raised you. Try to apply this into real life, by adding short compliments and try to brighten someone’s day every day.
