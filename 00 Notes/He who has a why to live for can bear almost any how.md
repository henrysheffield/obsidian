# He who has a why to live for can bear almost any how

>”There is so much wisdom in Nietzsche’s words, ‘He who has a *why* to live for can bear almost any *how*’”
>Nietzsche (pp.109)

Frankl refers to Nietzsche multiple times as something that allowed him to manage the unbearable circumstances of the concentration camp. Frankl champions the notion of having “a *why* to live” as the most important aspect of a human. [add more]
