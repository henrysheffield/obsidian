# Heritage

## Notes

Physical artifacts and intangible societal attributes that are passed over millennia

**Tangible**

Refers to physical objects / places
→Spear, Pyramids

**Intangible**

Non-physical aspects of culture
→Language, stories, myths, music, etc

> [!ABSTRACT]
> Heritage shapes our identity and understanding of the world
