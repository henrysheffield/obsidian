# Homozygous

## Notes

- You inherit the exact same version of a gene (allele) from **both** of your biological parents

**Homozygous Dominant** - Dominant trait that is the SAME pair of alleles: $BB$

**Homozygous Recessive** - Recessive trait that is the SAME pair of alleles: $bb$
