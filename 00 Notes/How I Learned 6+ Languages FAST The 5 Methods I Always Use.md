#  How I Learned 6+ Languages FAST The 5 Methods I Always Use

> [!SUMMARY] **Summary**
> Covers a polyglot on how she learnt languages. Some methods: 
> 
> **Shadowing** (watching a native speaker speak and repeat what they say)
> **Bidirectional Translation** (translate to English, wait, translate back to language)
> **High Frequency Words/Phrases** (most common words of language, memorise them)
> **Videos**
> **Active input** (writing, speaking)

# Strategies

## 1. Shadowing

- This is a strategy of watching a native speaker video and repeating what they say
- Very effective for pronunciation and speaking

## 2. Bidirectional Translation

### Steps
- Find a large slab of text in your target language, it should be challenging and somewhat long
- Translate it into your native language
- Wait a few hours / days
- Translate *back* into the original language
### Why it works

- Forces you to see grammar and vocabulary.

## 3. High-Frequency Words 

- Prioritise the top 100 most common adjectives / verbs in a language rather than random nouns e.g. fork
- Keep a printed list of your desk at all times, refer to them when writing, try to do it daily
- Makes memorising easy

## 4. Videos

- Watch Videos
- If you can understand ~60% or more that is a good level

## 5. Active Output

### What is it?
- Skills such as speaking or writing where you are constantly outputting information
### Strategies
**Speaking topic**: Pick a topic, record yourself speaking for 2 - 15 minutes. Listen back to the recording, catch mistakes and re-record.

→ The Youtuber said that she likes to stick to the same topic for a week or more to see her progress.

**Summary** - After input (listening, reading) try to summarise it verbally in your target language

**Micro-writing** - Writing should be a small and not too important skill, besides for exams. Small tasks like ***journalling or gratitude lists*** are highly beneficial.
