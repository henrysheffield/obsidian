### Summary

Stoicism is a philosophy to live a *eudaimonic* life–that is, the best life possible. The main way is to focus on what is *in our control* (how we react to things) and what is *not in our control* (exam results, other people). This way, we can concentrate on the good parts of life rather than focusing on the negatives. To apply these concepts in real life, we must employ these tactics in every situation (e.g. an argument) or opportunity we experience every day.
