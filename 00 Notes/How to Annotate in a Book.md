# How to Annotate

## Notes

**Highlighters**
- Only 1 or 2 are needed
- Blue- The core concepts and ideas
- Green - The evidence
- Pink (optional) - Words/vocab that you do not know\

**The Pen**
- Add **short** and quick annotations
- Use the following symbols for speed:

`*` - Asterisk - Absolutely essential, critical point to remember e.g. *glucose = $C_{6}H_{12}O_{6}$*
`?` - Question - You do not understand the concept / You want to clarify / Double check later
`->` - Arrow - To connect ideas, forming relations (crucial for obsidian)
## Examples / Problems

*"Acetaminophen is one of the most widely used analgesics worldwide, primarily indicated for the treatment of mild-to-moderate pain and fever. Unlike non-steroidal anti-inflammatory drugs (NSAIDs) such as ibuprofen, it possesses minimal anti-inflammatory activity. The exact mechanism of action remains complex, but it is heavily understood to act primarily in the central nervous system. It works largely by inhibiting cyclooxygenase (COX) enzymes, particularly variants in the brain, thereby reducing the synthesis of prostaglandins which are responsible for transmitting pain signals. It is rapidly absorbed in the gastrointestinal tract, with peak plasma concentrations occurring within 30 to 60 minutes after ingestion."*

**Blue (Core concept)**
`One of the most widely used analgesics worldwide ... for mild-to-moderate pain and fever`

**Green (Evidence)**
`It works largely by inhibitng cyclooxygenase (COX) enzymes ... reducing synthesis of prostaglandins, which are responsible for transmitting pain signals.`

**Pink (Optional - Words)Pen (Annotation)**
`Cyclooxygenase`

**Pen (Annotation)**
`Brain-related, -> unlike diclofenac (peripheral)`
