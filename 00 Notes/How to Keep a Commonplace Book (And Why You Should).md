# How to Keep a Commonplace Book (And Why You Should)

![How to Keep a Commonplace Book (And Why You Should)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aaHEgPk0tNM)

## Notes

- Write down quotes / ideas that you like 
- Use this book for future inspiration when you don’t know what to do
- Ideas do not have to be connected but you can look for relations

### Steps

1. Read / watch / talk
2. Capture 
	1. Write ideas down
	2. Include a source
	3. Ideas do not necessarily have to be awesome–embrace imperfections
3. Reflect
	1. Every once in awhile write in your book
	2. Consider what these ideas mean

> [!INTERESTING] 
> “Seneca and Aristotle used commonplace books for their ideas [3:30]
