# How to Read People like a Book Summary

## Notes

### When talking to someone:

- Pay attention to body language–small quircks, gestures or anything can be signals of someone’s emotions. 
	- Note: it can also be by chance so don’t fully commit to body language
- Perform [[Active Listening]]
- Be aware of your own biases – always meet people with an open mind

Have patience and practice this skill, it is difficult but incredibly rewarding!
