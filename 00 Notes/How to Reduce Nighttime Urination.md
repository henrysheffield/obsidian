 How to Reduce Nighttime Urination

**Summary:** This note documents how to reduce nighttime urination and how it works, getting the information from an Andrew Huberman video.

## Video

![](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9kkspssvcLc)

---
## Description

The video talks about how kidney circulation is highly regulated by circadium rhythms. For instance, it states that your kidney is most rapid and efficient in the first 10 hours of waking. Therefore, you should try to drink >90% of your water in this period and drink approximately 5 - 8 ounces after.
(Note waking up at 5:45 means: ==05:45 - 15:45 or 5:45 AM - 3:45 PM)==

## Key Ideas

- From the first 10 hours of waking, your kidneys filter at a very rapid rate
- After this, the kidney slows down, causing it to be harder for your body to process large amounts of water without needing to urinate

- Aim to ==consume your water in the first 10 hours==
- After the 10 hour mark have only around ==5 - 8 ounces (150 - 230 mL)== before bed
- Sip in evening, gulping causes body to excrete fluid more rapidly

## Examples / Problems

- Wake up at 5:45 AM, drink >90% of your daily water (usually under 3-4 L) *before* 15:45 (3:45 PM)
- Drink smaller sips of water beyond this period before bedtime (~5 - 8 ounces is good)
