# Care Guide: Boxing & Muay Thai Gloves

Proper glove mainteance is essential for hygiene and lengthening the life of your boxing gloves. 

---

### ## 1. Immediate Post-Training Care

Never leave your gloves in your gym bag. A dark, damp bag is a breeding ground for bacteria.

- **Exterior:** Wipe down with a damp cloth to remove sweat and blood.
    
- **Interior:** Use a **dry microfiber cloth** to soak up as much surface moisture as possible.
    
- **Disinfect:** Use a non-bleach disinfectant wipe or a mild vinegar-water solution to kill bacteria inside the glove.
    

### ## 2. The Drying Process

Proper airflow is more important than heat. In fact, excessive heat is the enemy of leather and synthetic materials.

- **Open Up:** Undo the Velcro straps to the maximum to allow the glove to "breathe."
    
- **Airflow:** Hang your gloves in a well-ventilated area (e.g., near a window or under a ceiling fan).
    
- **Deodorizers:** Insert glove dogs/deodorizers and push them all the way into the finger area.
    
- **Avoid:** * **Direct Sun:** UV rays crack the material.
    
    - **Hairdryers:** High heat damages the padding and dries out the leather.
        

### ## 3. Preventative Maintenance

- **Hand Wraps:** Always wear them. They act as a sponge for sweat, keeping it out of the glove lining.
    
- **Wash Wraps:** Clean your wraps after every single use to prevent transferring old bacteria back into your gloves.
    
- **Deodorizer Refresh:** Once a month, leave your **deodorizer inserts** (not the gloves) in the sun for a few hours to reactivate them and kill trapped odors.
    

---

### ## Daily Checklist

1. [ ] Wipe interior/exterior with microfiber and disinfectant.
    
2. [ ] Insert deodorizers and hang for airflow.
    
3. [ ] Wash hand wraps.
    
4. [ ] Pack clean gear and a towel for tomorrow's session.
