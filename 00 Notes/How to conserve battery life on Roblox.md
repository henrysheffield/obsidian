# How to conserve battery life on Roblox

## Notes

- Turn on battery saver / power efficiency **before** opening Roblox
- Inside a game, lower Roblox graphics to 1 - 3
- Also max FPS at 60
- Use `Ctrl + Shift + Esc` to close Roblox
- Lower screen brightness 30 - 50 %
