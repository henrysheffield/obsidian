# How to embed quotes

## Notes

- Do not ‘quote dump’, instead add short **impactful** quotes (3-8 words) seamlessly in one sentence to ensure flow
- Use [brackets] to change pronouns / tenses to ensure grammar
	- *’The speaker states that “[she] was terrified”’*
- Use elipses (…) to remove irrelevant parts of the quote

## Steps

### 1. Subject / Verb
- Weave quote in by using a signal verb (demonstrates, argues, suggests, conveys, reveals)
- *Rose’s claim of civic duty is revealed when Juror 8 states, “Let’s talk about it,” reinforcing his contention to …*

### 2. Comma 
- Before quote
- *As Juror 3 notes, “Rotten kid”*
- See more in [[Quote Grammar]]
### 3. Structural integration
- Maintain a literary flow
- *Reginald Rose demonstartes the justice system as “glass” hoping to convey the fragilness of the framework*
