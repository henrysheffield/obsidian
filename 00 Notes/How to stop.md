# How to stop

## Notes

- You don’t want to do it
- Change your attitude: *”I’m trying to quit” → “I’m done”*
	- Treat it as a lifestyle, not a trend
- No need to track days, just do it
- See **relapse** as feedback, not failure

## Tired, bored, not much to do. What is the best thing to do here?

- Go outside
- Train
- Journal
- Cold shower
- Meditation
- Read

Do anything that will make you proud, not guilty.

> [!TIP]
> Just focus on the person that you want to be

## Benefits

- Gain resilience
- Sharper focus
- Confidence

> [!QUOTE]
> *”I’m not doing it” → “I’m building self-control”*


## Steps

1. Identify your struggles (Mine would be urge)
2. Feedback from failures (It will happen, just write down why, how, action steps)
3. Replace habit: *meditation, cold showers, walking, reading …*
