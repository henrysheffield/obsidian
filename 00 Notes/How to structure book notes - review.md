# How to structure book notes - review

## Notes

- Instead of spending lots of time of separate notes, work heavily on the canvas
	- This way you can physically see the ideas as connected texts, not random notes
	
>[!MAIN IDEA] 
>Put multiple concepts into **the one book note** but for certain ideas that need more than a few dotpoints to elaborate on, create new notes *for those ideas*

- Important: you relate these notes in the book, NOT a MOC like [[Philosophy MOC]]
- Include a 3 sentence summary of the book and what you thought of it
