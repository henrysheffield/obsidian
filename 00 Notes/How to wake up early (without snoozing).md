# How to wake up early (without snoozing)

![How to wake up early (without snoozing)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hhPWHoWgu9g)

## Notes

> [!TIP]
> One of the best ways to start you day is just get out of bed **straight away** and do 20 pushups.


Waking up early allows you to have more energy, feel better and be more productive.

## Steps

**1. Remove Friction**

- Move alarm clock out of your reach
- Lay clothes night before (mandatory)
- Good routine example: `10 PM - 6 AM`

**2. As soon as the alarm rings, get out of bed immediately**

- Never debate with your brain
- Never go back to bed
- Just get out as fast as you can

**3. Do a workout immediately**

Can be:
- Pushups
- Jumping jacks
- Walking
- Light jogging


## Notes on routine document

**1. Move in first 20 minutes of waking**

- Jog/walk outside
- Yoga
- Pushups
- Jumping Jacks

**2. Reflect 20 minutes**

- Peace / Stillness
- Meditate
- Gratitude journalling
- ==Smile!==

**3. Plan 20 minutes**

- Use [[Eisenhower Matrix]] to divide tasks into 4 categories
- Do musts first and then go through other tasks

> [!TIP]
> **Use your notepad and diary every day to ensure you complete your todos–no days off.**
