#  I learnt a system for remembering everything (from books)

**Quick Summary:** Explains Ryan Holiday's note-taking system from books, he writes notes and creates flashcards while reading *slowly*.

![](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Rvey9g0VgY0&t=2s)

---
## Captured Notes

- Don't just *read* instead, perform active note-taking of the book, if it is a non-library book use a highlighter for key quotes and use page stickers to find the quotes.
- After a reading session Ryan Holiday would create physical flashcards (I would just do it digitally using Anki)
- ==Review review review== and take your time while reading
---
## Flashcards

When reading what should you be doing constantly to improve memory? :: Actively take notes, highlight and review what you have done. Moreover, creating flashcards for that book / chapter is essential.
