# Igneous Rocks

## Notes

- Formed by magma (molten rock)
- It is literally just cooled magma rock
- *Ignis* means *Fire* in latin

What does ignis mean in latin? :: Fire
<!--SR:!2026-09-25,81,350-->

### Intrusive
- Formed below Earth’s surface slowly
- Form large crystals
### Extrusive
- Formed above Earth’s surface
- Cools rapidly
- Can be vesicular (have lots of holes from bubbles) or glassy (like obsidian)
