# Integrity

## Notes

In stoicism, integrity is one of the most important aspects of a human.

>[!QUOTE]
>”But for a Stoic, there is no trade off between A and B: nothing can be traded if the price is the compromising of your character.”
>[[How To Be A Stoic]] - pp.76

You integrity is imperative in stoicism, your personality is everything.

→One of the reasons you must be [[Call strangers 'brother', 'sister' or even 'uncle'|kind to strangers all of the time.]]

> [!QUOTE]
> “The true value of a person lies in their core, and that core–our character–remains regardless of the role we happen to play in society.”
> [[How To Be A Stoic]] - pp.103

The personality of a person and who they are is everything for stoics. You can *never* “compromise your character”

→I believe this is what Stockdale applied when he became a POW in Vietnam
