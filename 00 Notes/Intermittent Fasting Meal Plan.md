Here is a highly optimized, repetitive meal plan designed specifically around your 1:05 PM lunch time and afternoon training schedule. It runs perfectly on an 8-hour window (1:00 PM to 9:00 PM) while leaving your mornings clear.

### 2. The "Once-A-Month" Bulk Prep Strategy

To completely remove cooking friction during the school week:

- **The Meat Freeze:** Buy 2-3kg of chicken breast and lean beef mince. Weigh them out into exact **180g (chicken)** and **150g (beef)** portions while raw. Place each portion into its own Ziploc bag or vacuum seal it. Flatten the bags so they stack in your freezer and thaw quickly.
    
- **The Night Before:** Move one bag of chicken from the freezer to the fridge to thaw.
    
- **The Morning Of:** Cook the chicken in a pan (5 minutes) while you get ready, throw it in your lunch container with some pre-cooked rice and frozen veggies. By 1:05 PM, the frozen veggies will have thawed perfectly and acted as an ice pack to keep your chicken safe.
    
- **Dinner Rotation:** Keep salmon fillets in the freezer. You can bake them straight from frozen with the sweet potato, or swap in one of your pre-portioned beef bags for a quick stir-fry.
