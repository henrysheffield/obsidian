# Intuitionism

## Notes

Knowledge / decisions are implanted in us before being birthed/as we are a baby.

Instinct is a core theme.

### Example

Chimpanzees generally aid another chimpanzee who is in moral or physical distress. Here, they are acting **instinctively**
