# Isomer

## Notes

A molecule with the same molecular formula (same number of atoms) but a different structural arrangement of the atoms.
## Example

![[Isomer-1781917943415.webp|700x260]]

This is the exact same molecule of but-2-ene. However, there are two different isomers, one where the carbons are on the same side (*cis*) and the second where the carbons are on different sides (*trans*)
