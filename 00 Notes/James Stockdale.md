# Note

## Notes

- American General
- Crashlanded in Vietnam during the war
- Served as a POW in the Hanoi Hilton
- Used [[Epictetus]] as comfort

> [!QUOTE]
> “Five years down there at the least. I’m leaving the world of technology and entering the world of Epictetus.”

Despite receving numerous beatings, torture and solitary confinement. Stockdale realised there was absolutely nothing he could do to [[Dichotomy|control]] the situation. Therefore, he remained faithful.

### The Stockdale Paradox

- Always keep the faith you have (your [[Integrity]])
- Always be completely honest about the harsh facts of your situation
