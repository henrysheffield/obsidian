# Keeping Teeth White

**Summary:** This note looks at methods for keeping your teeth white. Some important notes: ==never brush your teeth right after eating== and use ==fluoride toothpaste==. Finally, you should always floss.

## Key Ideas

## Stain Prevention

- Never brush your teeth immediately after eating, instead wait approximately 30 minutes so your saliva can remineralize the surface of your teeth.
- ==Wash your mouth after eating foods that stain (e.g. coffee, tea)==
- Use a straw often for staining liquids, this way, the liquid passes through your teeth.

## Maintenance

- Use an electric toothbrush as it makes it much easier to brush your teeth properly
- Use whitening toothpaste sparingly. 
	- This is because the toothpaste has high abrasivity and can damage your gums
	- Do not use daily as it can yellow your teeth over time
	
- Floss daily (it should be a non-negotiable part of your evening routine)

## Diet

- Apples, carrots and celery are natural toothbrushes, eat them often!

- **White Shirt Test** - If it would stain a white shirt then it would stain your teeth
	- Blueberries, soy sauce, turmeric are examples of food that stain

## How to Implement into your daily routine?

- Incorporate flossing into your morning routine every day (review this in a *daily note*)
- Do not use whitening toothpaste all the time. Instead stick with fluoride toothpaste, my favourite brand at the moment is *Sensodyne*
- Wash your mouth with mouthwash / water ==immediately== after drinking coffee or tea
→ Ideally you should swish for 60s and gargle for 30s
