# Kind to relations (family, friends, etc)

>”How have you behaved up to now towards gods, parents, brother, wife, children, teachers, tutors, friends, relations, servants? Has your principle been ‘say no evil, do no evil’?

Have you been kind, forgiving and never in anger with the people you love (Mum, Dad, brother, friends, grand-parents)?

This is a mandatory personality that you absolutely must adhere to always.
