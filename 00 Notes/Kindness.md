# Kindness

## Notes

Always, always be kind to people. Do lovely things for people you like, such as [[Kind to relations (family, friends, etc)|friends, family, grand-parents]].

Some methods to make others happy and for yourself to be more kind:

- Give a compliment to someone
- [[Active Listening]]
- Do the dishwasher
- Do the laundry
- Make a lovely meal 
- Make brownies and give them to everybody
