# Laptop battery problem

## Notes

- Last night I charged my laptop to a good amount (76%)
- I left the usb for my keyboard in
- Caused the laptop to make noise (fan) and die to 0% overnight

### How I fixed it

- This can happen again, but to fix it for the future: **just unplug the usb**
- This is the easiest strategy

### What I learned

- I also installed `tlp` as a powersaving tool
- Simply unplug any dongle before shutting your laptop
- Dark mode actually saves more battery than light mode (i thought it was the opposite!)
