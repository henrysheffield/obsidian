# Law of Conservation of Energy

## Notes

- Energy cannot be created nor destroyed
- Energy is **always** transferred from one form to another

$$\sum\limits E_{before}=\sum\limits E_{after}$$
*Mathematical statement showing that the sum of all energy before an event is identical to the amount of energy after the event*
## Examples / Problems

**Swinging Pendulum:** As a pendulum swings, it rapidly trades its **kinetic energy** (motion) for **potential energy** (height) and back again. At the very top of its swing, it stops and all energy is potential. At the lowest point, it moves at its fastest and all energy is kinetic.
