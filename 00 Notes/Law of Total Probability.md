# Law of Total Probability

## Notes

Suppose $B_{1} ... B_{k}$ are mutually exclusive and exhaustive events in a sample space. For any event A in the same space: $$P(A)=P(A \cap B_{1})+...+P(A\cap B_{k})=\sum_{i=1}^{k}{P(A\cap B_i)}$$
Note this is the exact same as: (this way is more useful)$$P(A)=P(B_1)P(A|B_1)+...+P(B_k)P(A|B_k)=\sum_{i=1}^{k}P(B_i)P(A|B_i)$$

## Core Conditions before use:

- Events must be [[Mutually Exclusive]] (cannot overlap)
- Must be [[Collectively Exhaustive]]
