# Learn How to Backflip in 5 Minutes  ASAP

![Learn How to Backflip in 5 Minutes | ASAP](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ltho8_PzC2U)

## Notes

1. Get used to jumping - Use full body (back, arms, legs, power jumps)
2. Power jump + knees right up
3. Backwards roll 
	1. Proper technique needed here
	2. Start slow, then get quicker and more powerful
	3. Get back to your feet straight away after roll
4. Stretch a bit to loosen up (loosen the spine e.g. cobra, bridge)
5. Do the *makaku*, using one arm, jump back (a half backflip)
6. Make the *makaku* more powerful
	1. Start to get more upside down
7. Fully commit
	1. Now you and the backflip went from *frenenemies* to *besties*
	2. Don’t be scared, just fully commit and DO IT!!!
