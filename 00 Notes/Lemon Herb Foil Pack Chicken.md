# Bulk-Batch Lemon Herb Foil Pack Chicken (2.1kg Scale)

Scaling up to 2.1 kilograms of chicken breast means you are prepping a significant volume of protein. To ensure the meat cooks evenly without stewing in too much piled-up moisture, it is highly recommended to split this into two separate foil packets baked side-by-side on a large tray, or on two separate shelves.

- **Total Time:** 26 to 28 minutes
    
- **Yield:** 10 to 12 portions
    
- **Cookware:** Heavy-duty aluminum foil and two large baking trays (or one extra-large tray)
    

---

## Ingredients

- 2.1 kg Chicken breasts, flattened to an even thickness
    
- 2 large Lemons, washed and cut into thin rounds
    
- 4 tablespoons Dried oregano
    
- 4 tablespoons Garlic powder
    
- 2 tablespoons Onion powder
    
- 4 tablespoons Olive oil
    
- 2 teaspoons Salt
    
- 2 teaspoons Black pepper
    

---

## Method

### 1. Spice and Divide

Preheat your oven to 200°C. Tear off two large sheets of heavy-duty aluminum foil and place them onto your baking trays. In a large bowl, toss the 2.1 kg of chicken breasts with the olive oil, garlic powder, onion powder, salt, and pepper until every piece is completely coated. Divide the chicken evenly between the two sheets of foil, arranging the breasts in a single layer with a small gap between each piece.

> **Visual Cue:** Layer the thin, bright yellow lemon rounds across the top of all the chicken breasts and scatter the dried oregano generously over the entire batch.

### 2. Double Seal the Packets

Bring the sides of the foil together over the meat for each batch. Fold the edges down tightly two or three times to create two distinct, well-sealed foil tents. Ensuring the seal is tight prevents steam from escaping, which is vital for keeping this volume of meat tender.

### 3. The Bulk Bake

Place the trays into the oven. Because of the increased mass of 2.1 kg of cold meat, bake at 200°C for 26 to 28 minutes. If you are using two different oven shelves, swap the top and bottom trays halfway through the cooking time to ensure an even bake. Remove from the oven and let the packets rest closed for 5 to 7 minutes.

> **Visual Cue:** Carefully open the foil pouches. You should see an abundance of golden, clear lemon-chicken broth bubbling at the bottom. The chicken breasts must be completely white and opaque throughout, and the lemon slices on top should look thoroughly softened and dark yellow.

---

## Meal Prep Tips

- **Avoid Crowding:** Do not attempt to stack the 2.1 kg of chicken on top of each other in a single pouch. Stacking causes the middle pieces to remain raw while the exterior pieces overcook. Keeping them in a flat, single layer across two packets guarantees uniform heat distribution.
    
- **Batch Freezing:** 2.1 kg yields a massive amount of food. If you cannot finish it all within 4 days, slice the cooked chicken, place it into airtight containers, pour a bit of the leftover foil juices over it to prevent freezer burn, and freeze for up to 3 months.
    
- **Shredding Option:** Because this bulk volume yields exceptionally juicy meat, it is perfect for shredding. Use two forks to pull the warm chicken apart right inside the foil pouch so it naturally drinks up all the seasoned lemon broth before you pack it away.
