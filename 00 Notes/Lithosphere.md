# Lithosphere

## Notes

- Solid and outer layer of the planet
- Contains crust and upper mantle
- Comes from Greek word *lithos* meaning *stone*

**Divided into:**

**Oceanic:** Lithosphere that is found underneath the ocean. It is more dense than continental.
**Continental:** Lithosphere found under land continents ~34 km thick

- All earthquakes occur in Lithosphere
- Divided into [[Tectonic Plates]]

What is the lithosphere? :: The lithosphere is the rigid, rocky outer shell of a planet or moon. On Earth, it comprises the entire crust and the brittle, topmost portion of the upper mantle. Averaging about 100 kilometers (60 miles) in thickness, it is the layer that forms our continents, ocean floors, and tectonic plates.
