# Logarithms

## Notes

Mathematical inverse of exponentiation.

![[Logarithm-1781312764755.webp]]



## Examples / Problems

When using logarithms we are asking for what value of $n$ in $a^n=b$ allows us to get b as an answer?

$$\log_{10}(100)=2<-->10^2=100$$

Solve for x:

$$\log_{3}(x+1)=3-\log_{3}(x+7)$$
