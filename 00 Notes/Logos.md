# Logos

## Notes

Stoic concept where the universe is not here by chance, instead it is an intelligent, ordered and coherent entity (*pantheistic - god IS the universe)*.

> [!QUOTE]
> “what they [stoics] called the *Logos* which can be interpreted as either God … or the simple fact that Nature is understandable by reason.”
> [[How To Be A Stoic]] - pp.6

### 1. Cosmic Logos

Universe did not come here by chance, instead, it is a single, living entity. The cosmic *logos* is behind it.

- **Synonyms:** It is often equated with _Nature_, [[Providence]], _Fate_, or even _God_.
- **Function:** It dictates the natural laws of science, the passage of time, and the interconnected web of cause and effect.

### 2. Individual Logos (Humans)

- Humans are special, we have a small fragment of the *logos* inside the mind
- Allows us to think critically

**Ultimate goal:** Align our thoughts and actions (Individual Logos) with the Cosmic Logos (the way the universe naturally unfolds by *following nature / letting fate guide our path*.

**Example:** [[Viktor E. Frankl]] let fate guide him, he didn’t purposefully go to Auschwitz, instead he remained in his homeland Austria, letting fate guide his path rather than choosing his own way.
