# London Dispersion Forces

## Notes

Temporary intermolecular forces that occur between atoms or molecules. Due to electrons being at random places at random times, a group of electrons can go to one side of the molecule, creating an [[Dipole|instantaneous dipole]], causing an attraction force. 

## Key properties

- Weak
- Temporary
- Intermolecular (between atoms or molecules)
- Occurs to [[Non Polar]] substances
