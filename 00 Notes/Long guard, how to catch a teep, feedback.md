## Notes
### To Practice
- Practice long guard + teep at the start of a sparring match
- Keep chin tucked
- Practice blocking teeps with arm
### Tips
- If somebody catches your teep →twist your foot sideways alongside your body and pull
- Catch a kick (on your left side) →right step very far back, left back, bring the foot to your wrist, fling the leg.

### What I learnt
- If somebody catches your kick, you can bend your leg, bringing them closer and then push away
