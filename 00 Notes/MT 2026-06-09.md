## Notes

We practiced on baiting the [[Teep]] from the padholder
→You basically push your stomach out a bit to bait a teep, when the person teeps, move your hip back and then do a combo

Also practiced on baiting a head combo 
→You move your head out a bit to make it easy to jab, kick or anything else
→When padholder goes for it, lean back straight away and do a combo (*e.g. power cross*)
