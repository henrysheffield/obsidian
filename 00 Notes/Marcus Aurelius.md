# Marcus Aurelius

## Notes

- Roman Emperor
- Constantly practiced the stoic principles throughout his life.

> [!QUOTE]
> “Of his thirteen children, only one son and four daughters outlived their father.”

Marcus Aurelius had “thirteen children” of which only “one son and four daughters” actually survived longer than he did. This is a depressing reality that Aurelius faced, who nevertheless pursued his lifelong goal of running an empire. This shows a man who was determined and would be *indifferent to pain and pleasure*, constantly reminding himself of the reality of [[Mortality]].
