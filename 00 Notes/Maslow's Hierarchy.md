# Maslow's Hierarchy

> [!TIP] **Summary**
> Maslow’s hierarchy of needs is a motivational theory in psychology comprising a five-tier model of human needs, often depicted as hierarchical levels within a pyramid.

## Description

Maslow’s hierarchy states the levels of human satisfaction. The bottom of the pyramid must be satisfied before the next stages. 

For example, having enough food is more important than status in society. 

## Key Ideas

- Not exactly linear, studies have shown that people can go through different stages of the pyramid at once.
- Changes with culture and norms
