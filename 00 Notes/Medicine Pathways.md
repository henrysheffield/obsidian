# Note

## Notes

### Requirements (for VCE)

- Minimum 30 in chemistry
- Minimum 30 in english
- A maths subject (methods or specalist) is preferred

### University Clinical Aptitude Test (UCAT)

- 2 hour computer based test
- July during year 12
- Tests logic, reasoning and speed
- Sign up in around March, do not forget it!

## Methods of entry
### 1. Direct entry (Monash) - The fastest route 

Makes medicine take about 5 years or more.
Guaranteed medicine straight away *if accepted*

**ATAR:** `>99.4` (but really `>99.7`)
**UCAT:** `>94th percentile`
**Interview (MMI)** - `High Score`

### 2. Graduate entry

- 3 year undergraduate (e.g. biomedicine, science or something else)
- Apply for a 4 year Doctor of Medicine (MD)

### 3. Guaranteed Entry (Chancellor Scholarship)

Requires `>99.90 ATAR` 

Only valid if you maintain a weighted average mark (WAM) of `>75%` AND pass Multi Mini Interview, your spot is guaranteed

### 4. Standard Entry

High school scores do not matter–you start completely fresh in university

**Admission based on:**
1. **GPA / WAM** - Average grade across 3 years = `6.5 - 7 (basically straight As)`
2. **GAMSAT** - `4.5 Hour exam: reasoning, essay, high level biology sciences`
3. **MMI Interview** - `Evaluates communication and empathy`

### 5. Studying abroad (French, least good option)

- Affordable BUT very competitive
- In France, you study for one year, take an exam and then 8% of the top people actually get in
- You must deal with the language and cultural barrier
- If you take back your certificates to Australia, domestic medicine is priorities, meaning it is harder to find medicine jobs in Australia
- Not really worth it for the time and effort compared to studying in Australia
