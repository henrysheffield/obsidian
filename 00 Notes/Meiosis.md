# Meiosis

## Notes

Type of cell division for [[Gametes|gametes]] where one cell divides into 4 genetically different cells.

Split into 2 main parts (Meiosis I & II) with 4 sub-parts 

## Meiosis I (PMAT)

### 1. Prophase I

- Chromatin condenses into sister chromatids (connected by a centromere)
- [[Homologous Chromosomes]] pair with each other (called **synapsis**), forming a **tetrad** (4 sister chromatids)
- During this period, the chromosomes **cross over**–meaning they swap certain [[Alleles|alleles]] at random, this causes different genetic traits in the gametes
- Nuclear membrane disappears

![[Meiosis-1782275638147.webp]]
*Diagram of how crossing over works*

### 2. Metaphase I

- Chromosomes go to equator of the cell
- Centrioles with spindle fibres are introduced
- Spindle fibres attach to the chromosomes

### 3. Anaphase I

- The [[Homologous Chromosomes]] are separated, and are dragged to opposite poles of the cell
- They form a ‘V’ shape as they move through the cytoplasm
- The middle of the cell is slowly pinched

### 4. Telophase I

- Nuclear membrane reforms around chromosomes
- Spindle fibres disappear
- Cell breaks apart into two due to **cytokenisis**


## Meiosis II (MAT)

### 1. Metaphase

- Nuclear membrane disappears
- Spindle rods and centrioles introduced
- Chromosomes go to equator of cell
- Spindle rods attach to chromosomes

### 2. Anaphase

- Spindle rods drag the sister chromatids apart
- Forms a ‘V’ shape
- Note: the sister chromatids are different due to the *crossing over* in Meiosis I

### 3. Telophase

- Nuclear membrane forms around the chromatids
- Cell splits into two due to **cytokenisis**

### Result

Four genetically different gametes formed
