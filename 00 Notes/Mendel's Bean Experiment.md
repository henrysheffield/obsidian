# Mendel’s Bean Experiment

## Notes

- In 19th century wanted to study genetics
- Chose the bean plant because it was easy to breed quickly and study the different characteristics of the beans.
## Example #1: Flower colour

- Mendel found that the purple colour was the **dominant** [[Genotype & Phenotype|phenotype]] whereas, the white colour was recessive
- Found if a [[Heterozygous|heterozygous]] combination of a purple flower + white flower, the first generation will always be the dominant trait (purple) but the second generation would be random.
