# Metals

## Notes

- Elements that like to lose electrons, forming cations (positively charged ions)
- Contain a ‘sea’ of **delocalised electrons**
- When metals heated, the cations in the metal [[Lattice]] vibrate more vigorously as their thermal energy increases (think of the example in class)

> [!TIP]
> The **delocalised electrons** (free moving electrons) allow for electricity and heat to easily flow through. Therefore, the metal has high conductivity

![[Metals-1784353401781.webp]]
### Properties

- Low electronegativity
- High melting point
- High boiling point
- Lusturous (shiny)
- Ductile (can be formed into wires)
- Malleable (can be formed into thin sheets)
