# Metamorphic Rocks

## Notes

- Originate from [[Sedimentary Rocks]] and [[Igneous Rocks]] which undergo extreme temperature and pressure
	- Usually subjected to temperatures above $100 - 150^{\circ} C$ and above 100 mega pascals, causing profound physcial changes. [^1]

> [!TIP]
> *Protolith* refers to the original rock that a metamorphic once was 
> e.g. Limestone →Marble

**Classified as:**

[[Foliation|Foliated]] - Visible layers on the rock
Non-Foliated - A bit ugly, no layers
