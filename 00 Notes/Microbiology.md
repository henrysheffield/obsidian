# Microbiology

Microbiology centres around [[Bacteria]] which are tiny organisms that are made out of [[Cell|cells]], of which there are two main types: [[Eukaryotic]] and [[Prokaryotic]]. 

Bacteria can be divided into [[Gram Positive Bacteria]] and [[Gram Negative Bacteria]] which can be seen via [[Gram Staining]]

[[Staphylococcus Aureus]] - Example of bacteria

Cells contain several smaller [[Organelle|organelles]] (smaller specialised things that do functions). Some examples include: [[Mitochondria]], [[Cytoskeleton]], etc

[[Meiosis]] and [[Mitosis]] are fundamental concepts on how cells reproduce.
