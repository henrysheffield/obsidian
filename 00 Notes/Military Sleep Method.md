# Military Sleep Method

**Summary:** The military sleep method is a method to go to sleep within 2 minutes. It involves 2 main steps: 

1) Relaxing your entire body, focusing on each body part and working your way from the head to feet 
2) Imagining yourself in a calm place (e.g. canoe in a lake). 

If thoughts arise simply repeat *'Don't think'* for 10 seconds

---
## Description

The Military Sleep Method focuses on a progressive 'shut down' of your nervous system. If done correctly, one can sleep within 2 minutes.

## Key Ideas

- Relax your nervous system and body parts
- After relaxing, imagine yourself in a canoe in a still lake with a blue sky and nothing else
- If thoughts arise, simply repeat *'Don't think'* for 10 seconds

## Steps

1. Start with the face: Close your eyes, relax forehead, eye sockets, jaw and tongue.
2. Shoulders and hands: let your shoulders drop as low as they can go, relax your upper arms, forearms then fingers
3. Breath - Exhale and release your chest, do this if thoughts arise
4. Legs - Relax your thighs, calves, ankles and finally, feet
5. Imagine yourself in one of two scenarios: 
	1. Lying in a canoe in a lake with nothing but a blue sky 
	2. Lying in a comfortable hammock in a pitch black room
6. If thoughts arise: Repeat 'Don't think' for 10 seconds

## How will I consistently implement this?

1. No-Movement Rule: Commit yourself to not moving once you start the method. No side sleeping, itching your nose or anything.
2. Pair it with the obsidian daily note.
3. If necessary: practice during the day (the military would practice in chairs)
