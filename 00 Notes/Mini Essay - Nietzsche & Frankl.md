Through ‘Man’s Search for Meaning’, Viktor E. Frankl champions the ==notion of reason==[^5] being a core necessity for ==life==[^6]. During his unbearable, appalling experience in Nazi concentration camps, ==Frankl often was comforted by Nietzsche==[^3], ==who==[^1] “He who has a why to live for can bear almost any how,” illustrating that it is imperative to have a meaning to live. Without meaning, Frankl warns us that faith in life can fade. This was true in so many prisoners of Auschwitz, who one day, “would reach for a ==cigarette==[^4]” this act was ==so uncommon==[^7] ==,==[^2] that it signified that the inmate would die within “48 hours.” Here, Frankl demonstrates the importance of meaning in one’s life, without it, we lose hope. However, by being inspired to live, man can continue to pursue his life, just as the author did, regardless of the conditions he was in. Ultimately, obtaining a powerful reason to live permits someone to endure the most terrible circumstances without giving up.

### Improved paragraph

Through _Man’s Search for Meaning_, Viktor E. Frankl champions the notion that personal purpose is a core necessity for human survival. During his harrowing experience in Nazi concentration camps, Frankl found solace in Nietzschean philosophy, specifically the maxim that **“he who has a why to live for can bear almost any how”**; this illustrates the imperative need for psychological fortitude in the face of suffering. Frankl warns that without a definitive meaning, an individual's existential faith inevitably fades. This psychological collapse is epitomised by the prisoners of Auschwitz who, having lost all hope, **“would reach for a cigarette”**—a rare act of self-indulgence that served as a grim manifestation of submission, reliably predicting that the inmate would perish **“within 48 hours.”** Through this anecdote, Frankl demonstrates that the absence of meaning directly correlates to a loss of vitality. Ultimately, Frankl asserts that cultivating a powerful, internal reason to live grants individuals the resilience to endure the most terrible external circumstances without capitulating.
