# Mini Essays - The Perfect Tool for Lifelong Learners

![Mini Essays - The Perfect Tool for Lifelong Learners](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hi8meiPUt84)
## Summary

## Notes

**Mini essays:** Short essays (200-500 words) about one single concept or idea. Should take about 5-20 minutes to write.

- Strategy shows your understanding of a topic by explaining it in a short essay
- Relates to the [[Feynman Technique]] where you must: explain the concept simply and refine.
- Similar to social media, mini essays can be fun, as there is always a new concept to learn

## Steps

1. Keep on one single, simple idea. Over complicating it makes it too difficult and boring to understand.
2. Keep it short (200 - 500 words) or just about fits on a monitor screen.
3. Organise for retrieval. Use obsidian for the notes. 
4. **Include references** at the bottom of the essay which allows you to see where the information came from and allows for re-discovery ==(CRUCIAL)==

## Example

[["Every hour of the day give vigorous attention ... As it was the last of your life"]] - This is my first mini essay, a quote that I found meaningful in [[Meditations]]. I analysed it, and probably spent too long typing it, next time, set a maximum timer for 20 minutes for a mini essay.

## Application

- [x] When you learn a new concept, write a mini essay about it. This can be from school, books, general videos, etc. ✅ 2026-05-29
