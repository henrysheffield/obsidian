# Mitochondria

## Notes

Membrane bound organelles found in the cells of plants, animals and fungi. Known as ‘powerhouses of the cell’

Generate energy ([[ATP]]) for the cell.

Some mitochondria contain [[DNA|mitochondrial DNA]] because they evolved from ancient, free-living [[Bacteria|bacteria]].
