# Mitochondrion

## Notes

[[Organelle]] of the cell which is the **energy powerhouse**.
Creates energy ([[ATP]]) via cellular respiration.
Contains its own [[DNA]] called mitochondrial DNA (or mtDNA)
Found in majority of [[Eukaryotic]] cells (e.g. animals, plants, fungi)
