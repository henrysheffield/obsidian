# Mitosis

## Notes

A process where a somatic cell undergoes cell division to divide into two different cells.
It is divided into four parts (PMAT)

![[Mitosis-1782276118030.webp]]
*My diagram of Mitosis*

### 1. Prophase

- Chromatin condenses into [[Chromosomes]]
- Nucleolus dissapears
- [[Centriole|Centrioles]] with spindle fibres are introduced
- Spindle fibres attach to chromosomes

### 2. Metaphase

- [[Chromosomes]] go to equator of the cell

### 3. Anaphase

- The sister chromatids in the chromosone are separated 
- Dragged to opposite poles of the cell
- Middle of the cell slowly begins to be pinched

### 4. Telophase

- The [[Centriole]] and spindle fibres disappear
- Nuclear membrane surrounds the sister chromatids
- Nucleolus visible

### Cytokenisis

- Cell splits into two
- Chromatids spread out into chromatin
