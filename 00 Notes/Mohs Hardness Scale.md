# Mohs Hardness Scale

## Notes

- Similar to a [[Logarithm|Logarithmic]] hardness scale **but not entirely logarithmic** for rocks (0 - 10)
- Large jumps in hardness through the rocks 
- Developed in 1812 by German Friedrich Mohs
- Ranges from 1 (soft talc) to 10 (hard diamond)
- The Mohs scale is an [ordinal scale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ordinal_scale "Ordinal scale"). For example, [corundum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corundum "Corundum") (9) is twice as hard as [topaz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Topaz "Topaz") (8), but diamond (10) is about four times as hard as corundum, for absolute hardness.[[10]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mohs_scale#cite_note-11)
