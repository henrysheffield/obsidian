# Molar Mass

## Notes

The mass of one [[Mole|mole]] of a substance, measured in $\text{g/mol}$

It is the same as atomic mass unit ($\text{amu}$)

Example: Carbon has an atomic mass of $12.011~ \text{amu}$ so it has $12.011 ~\text{g/mol}$ molar mass.

$$M = \frac{m}{n}$$
*where*

$M$ - Molar Mass ($\text{g/mol}$)
$m$ - Mass of substance ($g$)
$n$ - Number of moles of substance ($mol$)

### Example: Find the molar mass of $H_{2}O$

To find the molar mass of water, sum the masses of its constituents:

2. **Hydrogen (H):** $1.008 \times 2 = 2.016$
    
3. **Oxygen (O):** $16.00 \times 1 = 16.00$
    
4. **Total:** $2.016 + 16.00 = 18.016$ **g/mol**
