# Molarity 

## Notes

Fundamental unit in chemistry, describes amount of substance (solute) dissolved in a specific volume of liquid (solvent).

$$M = \frac{n}{V}$$
**$M$**: Molarity (Units: $mol/L$ or simply **M**)
    
 **$n$**: Number of [[Mole|moles]] of solute ($mol$)
    
 **$V$**: Total volume of the solution in **Liters** ($L$)
