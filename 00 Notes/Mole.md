# Mole

## Notes

Fundamental SI unit to measure the amount of a substance. It is simply a counting unit to represent a specific number of something.

$$\text{1 mol}=6.022 \times 10^{23} \text{ particles}$$

## Examples / Problems

How many atoms are in 2 moles of Helium? :: The Helium part does not matter, we just care that it is an element: $6.022 \times 10^{23}~atoms/mol \times 2~mol=12.04 \times 10^{24}~atoms$
