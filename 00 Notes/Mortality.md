# Mortality

## Notes

Mortality is the realisation that we will all inevitably die soon.

We live for a mere fragment of time in the universe, therefore we must make the most of our time while we are here.

> [!QUOTE]
> “Memento Mori”
> Latin for ‘Remember you will die’

The above phrase is an ancient roman saying to showcase the urgency to fulfil tasks and live life to the fullest extent.
