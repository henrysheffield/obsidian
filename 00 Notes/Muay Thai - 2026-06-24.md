## Notes

- While boxing Vinh advised me to keep guard up very high up whenever you are in the ‘danger zone’ of an opponent
- Always apply this when you are close enough for an opponent to punch you when boxing bag and sparring.
- Allowed to drop hands when far away (e.g. to teep)

In short:

When in ‘danger zone’ of person / bag, always keep guard high–you should never have to bring arm/hand up to guard when somebody throws a punch at you.
