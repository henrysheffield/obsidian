## Notes

Learned technique of:

### 1. Somebody measures → you step around them

- Opponent measures
- Place left foot to the side of the person
- Twist 90 degrees
- Throw a quick combo (e.g. jab hook)

### 2. Somebody measuring → you make space

- Take a half step with your left foot (DO NOT CROSS YOUR LEGS HERE)
- You can go backwards with your right foot, making space 
- Or you can push your right foot out and twist then throw a combo
- If you are fast and have enough space → kick else → punch/combo
