## Description

Rule allowing you to find probability of two events. Changes according to type (see below)

## [[Independent Event]]

$$ P(A \cap B) = P(A) \times P(B) $$
A and B are independent (e.g. a coin toss) so knowing the outcome of one of the events does not affect the chance of another event

## [[Dependent Event]]

$$ P(A \cap B) = P(A) \times P(B | A) $$

Formula changes as knowing the outcome of one of the events changes the probability
