# Never give up

## Notes

Singlehandedly the most important part of life, you need to stay true to your goals and not give up.

> [!QUOTE]
> “I had no intention of losing hope and giving up. For no man knew what the future would bring, much less the next hour.”
> pp.90

Frankl was in an ultra uncertain time, with no set period of his imprisonment. Nevertheless, he pursued, fulfilled his tasks, never gave up, **and never complained (maybe).** 

Apply when you are working on things (e.g. french–never give up, or your hobbies)
