# Never regard something as a benefit to you

> “Never regard as a benefit to yourself anything … to hate, suspect or curse another.”
> 3.7

Respect and be kind to others. Never engage with other people as a means for benefiting yourself (e.g. conversation) instead just enjoy. ==* analyse further==
