# Nikolay Gogol

## Notes

- Born in 1809 in Poltava Province, Ukraine
- Russian novelist and playwright
- Gogol used the technique of *defamiliarization*[^1] whereby an author presents common things or objects in an unfamiliar or strange way, creating a new perspective on the world
- After 1836 he lived mainly abroad, insisting that writing about Russia required distance away from the land [^2]
- After 1840s fell into a deep traumatic period, burned much of Part II of [[Dead Souls]]
- Heavily influenced by a fanatic priest and subjected himself to extreme fasting as an effort to cleanse his sins–this destroyed his health[^3]
- Died in 1852 (aged 42)
