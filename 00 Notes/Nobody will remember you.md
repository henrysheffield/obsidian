# Nobody will remember you

> “Words in common use long ago are obsolete now. So too the names of those once farmed are in a sense obsolete - Camillus, Caeso, Volesus, Dentatus; a little later Scipio and Cato, then Augustus too, then Hadrian and Antonius. **All things fade and quickly turn into myth.**”

This statement looks at the bigger picture, over just a few decades or centuries, powerful emperors are completely forgotten in society–except in history book.
→ How many roman names from that extract did you understand?

**Smaller picture**
People will probably forget your actions in a couple of minutes, if you did something crazy–it might last for a couple of months. After that short period in the grand scheme of things, it is all forgotten.

**So what?**

Do crazy things, nobody cares, nobody will remember. Don’t fall into [[Judgement]]
