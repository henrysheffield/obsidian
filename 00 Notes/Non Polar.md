# Non Polar

## Notes

Non [[Polar|polar]] is when a molecule **does not** have [[Dipole|dipoles]]. This means that electrons are shared equally around the molecule.

Generally non polar substances dissolve in other non polar substances, likewise polar substances tend to dissolve in polar substances.
## Examples / Problems

### 1. Hexane

Symmetrical structure and nearly identical electronegativities of carbon and hydrogen.
### 2. Carbon Dioxide

This molecule is non polar even though there is an electronegativity difference. However, the geometry is linear and symmetrical, meaning that it is non polar.
