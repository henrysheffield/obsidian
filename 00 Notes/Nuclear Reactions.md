## Nuclear Reactions

> [!TIP] **Summary**
> Nuclear reactions come in 4 main forms: Alpha (emits a helium atom), Beta-minus (emits an electron), Beta-plus (emits a positron) and Gamma (a photon)

### Description

To perform a nuclear reaction you must:
1. Balance Atomic Mass number
2. Balance Atomic number

![[Nuclear Reactions-1777792450870.webp]]

### Types of radiation equations

![[Drawing 2026-05-03 16.27.18.excalidraw]]
[[Alpha Radiation]]

- Emits 2 neutrons + 2 protons (Helium atom)

![[Drawing 2026-05-03 16.29.07.excalidraw]]
[[Beta Radiation]]

![[Nuclear Reactions-1778395168980.webp]]

- Above is [[Beta Radiation]] (releases an electron = antineutrino)
- Occurs when ratio of neutrons to protons is unstable
- most common form is beta decay (where a neutron turns into a proton + electron)

![[Drawing 2026-05-03 16.30.49.excalidraw]]
[[Gamma Radiation]]

- Release of energy from ‘excited nucleus’ but no change in original atom

### Comparison Table

| **Type**  | **Particle/Wave**  | **Notation**                     | **Mass Change** | **Atomic Number Change** |
| --------- | ------------------ | -------------------------------- | --------------- | ------------------------ |
| **Alpha** | Helium Nucleus     | ${^{4}_{2}He}$                   | -4              | -2                       |
| **Beta**  | Electron/Positron  | ${^{0}_{-1}e}$ or ${^{0}_{+1}e}$ | 0               | +1 / -1                  |
| **Gamma** | High-energy Photon | ${^{0}_{0}\gamma}$               | 0               | 0                        |
