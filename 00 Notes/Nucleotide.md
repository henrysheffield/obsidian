# Nucleotide

## Notes

Fundamental building block of [[DNA]] and [[RNA]]

![[Nucleotide-1782118531884.webp]]
*A phosphate group paired with a sugar group and a nitrogenous base.*

**Contains:**

1. Phosphate group
2. Pentose sugar (deoxyribose for DNA, ribose for RNA–meaning including the hydroxyl group)
3. Nitrogenous base (Adenine, Thymine/Urecil, Guanine and Cytosine)

>[!NOTE]
>Adenine and Guanine are purines–meaning a double ring structure (like in the image above)
>Thymine and Cytosine are pyrimidines–meaning a single ring structure.
