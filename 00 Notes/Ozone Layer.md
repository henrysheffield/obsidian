## Description

The Ozone layer is a crucial layer in the Stratosphere (located in the [[Atmosphere]]) that absorbs 97-99% of harmful UV-B Radiation. It is essential for living organisms as it prevents the radiation.
## Key Ideas

- Located 15-35 km above surface
- Located in the Stratosphere
- Absorbs 97 - 99% of UV-B
- Acts as a protective shield
- Stratospheric Ozone is good as it protects life
- Tropospheric is bad, ozone can be a harmful pollutant and component of smog
