# Penicillin

Penicillins are a group of $\beta$-lactam antibiotics[^1] originally found in mold. It is a widely used [[Antibiotics]] to treat [[Bacteria]] infections 


**Natural Penicillin**

- From organic sources
- Examples: *penicillin G, penicillin V*

**Semi-Synthetic Penicillin**

- Scientists alter penicillin’s natural form to make it more effective on select treatments
- Example: *Aminopenicillins* - Amoxicillin is one of the most common types of penicillin [^2]
