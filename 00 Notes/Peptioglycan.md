# Peptioglycan

## Notes

- Rigid, mesh-like polymer that forms the cell wall of most bacteria
- Particularly present in [[Gram Positive Bacteria]] which has thick cell walls made of lots of peptiglycan
- Acts as a protective exoskeleton for bacteria
