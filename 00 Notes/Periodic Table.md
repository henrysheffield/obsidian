# Periodic Table

## Notes

Table of the elements in the world, ordered in groups (columns) which dictate number of valence electrons (e.g. group 1 = 1 valence electron in outermost shell) and in periods (rows).

![[Periodic Table-1782608328024.webp]]
*Trends*

### Ionisation energy

Amount of energy required to remove an electron from an atom’s outermost shell.

Increases up and right

*e.g. $He$ requires the most ionisation energy due to a small nucleus with strong attractions to its electrons*
### Electron affinity 

How much an atom wants to gain and electron, increases up and right
### Atomic Radius

How large the atom is, with Francium being the largest.

Increases down and left
*because attraction between valence electrons increases → atoms become more compact → size decreases*

### Electronegativity

Measure of an atom’s ability to attract shared electrons toward itself with a chemical bond

Increases up and right

Fluorine is the most electronegative element
