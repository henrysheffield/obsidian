# Phospholipid

## Notes

- Amphipathic molecule (meaning: both hydrophobic and hydrophilic)
- Contains a head (hydrophilic)
- Two tails (hydrophobic)
- Group of phospholipids form a semi-permeable barrier in a cell membrane

### Head

- Phosphate group
- Glycerol
- [[Polar]] (negative charge)
- Hydrophilic (forms [[Hydrogen Bonding|hydrogen bonds]] with water)

### Tails

- Saturated tail (straight)
- Unsaturated tail
	- Contains a double bond → kink
