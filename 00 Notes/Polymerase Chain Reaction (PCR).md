# Polymerase Chain Reaction (PCR)

## Notes

Laboratory technique to make many copies of a single DNA segment. Allows for easy analyse of a small DNA fragment.

![[Polymerase Chain Reaction (PCR)-1783213434023.webp]]
### Steps

> [!TIP]
> Centrifuge is often used on samples to prevent air bubbles. This is why the plate is sealed with a sticky tape

#### 1. Denaturing

- Sample heated to $95^{\circ}$ 
- Causes double helix structure to separate
- Result: two separate strands
#### 2. Annealing

- Sample cooled to $55^{\circ}$
- Short segments of DNA (primers) bind to a specific part of the strand

#### 3. Extension / Elongation

- Heated to $72^{\circ}$
- An enzyme (TAQ Polymerase) binds to the primers, builds new strands (in 5’ to 3’ direction)
- Amount of DNA doubled
