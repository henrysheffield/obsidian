# Precision

## Notes

How consistent the results are, regardless of the [[Accuracy|accuracy]]. Generally, a [[Systematic Error|systematic error]] will have a high precision but low accuracy (how close it is to the real value).
## Examples / Problems

Imagine precision as darts far from a bullseye in a small cluster (because the experiment had errors). Whereas, accuracy is at random spots NEAR the bullseye.
