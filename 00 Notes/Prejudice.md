# Prejudice

## Notes

An unjustified, stereotypical, usually negative view of a certain person based on their social group. Often manifests a preconceived view of somebody before meeting, talking or experiencing life with them.

Particularly present in many classics such as

[[Twelve Angry Men]]

[[To Kill a Mockingbird]]

[[The Great Gatsby]]
