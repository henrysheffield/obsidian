# Probe

## Notes

Short, single stranded chain of nucleic acids.

Contains a: 

### 1. Reporter

- Gets excited → emits fluorescense light
- VIC - FAM most common

### 2. Quencher

- Suppresses the fluorescense of the reporter
- Can only do this when reporter and quencher are in close proximity
