# Proteins

## Notes

- Made of a chain of [[Amino Acids]]
- Amino acids bond together using **peptide bonds**
	→This is called a *condensation reaction* where water is lost

### Structures

![[Proteins-1782959250764.webp]]
*Primary, Secondary, Tertiary, Quartenary*
#### 1. Primary

Based on sequence of amino acids in a protein
One change in amino acids completely changes the protein

						Ala - Leu - Met
						Or: ALM
#### 2. Secondary

- Localised shape of protein
- Can be 1) Alpha helix or 2) Beta Sheet
- [[Hydrogen Bonding|Hydrogen bonds connect them]]

#### 3. Tertiary 

- 3D Folding pattern
- Consists of only one subunit

#### 4. Quartenary
- multiple subunits
- very complex
- e.g. Hemoglobin
