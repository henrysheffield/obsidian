# Quote Grammar

## Notes

Commas and periods almost always go **inside quotes**

Use brackets [ ] to change pronouns/tenses to ensure proper grammar
→*The speaker states that [she] was terrified*

Use an ellipses (…) to remove irrelevant parts of the quote
→*According to [[Twelve Angry Men|Juror 8]], “the most difficult thing to … [[Prejudice|prejudice]] always obscures truth.”*

The following table describes the basics of quote grammar, note the highlights showcasing commas, periods or quotes.
