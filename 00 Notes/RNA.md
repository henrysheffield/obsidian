# RNA

## Notes

RNA (*Ribonucleic acid*) is one of two nucleic acids (the other being [[DNA]]).

Contains a **ribose** sugar (includes a hydroxyl group).

Contains 4 nitrogenous bases: Adenine (A), **Urecil (U)**, Cytosine (C), Guanine (G)

Single stranded

RNA can be found in the cytoplasm (not locked in the nucleus)

Some [[Viruses]] contain RNA instead of DNA

## 3 Types

### 1. Messager (mRNA)

- Begins in nucleus
- Transcribes instructions from a portion of DNA
- Transports to ribosomal RNA 
### 2. Ribosomal RNA (rRNA)
- Site where instructions are read and translated into a protein
### 3. Transport RNA (tRNA)
- Transports [[Amino Acids]] which are the building blocks for the proteins
