# Recognise you good, bad and wild traits

> “Examine yourself … What sort of soul do I have after all? Is it that of a child? A boy? A woman? A despot? A beast of the field? A wild animal?”

Am I really in control of a certain action, or was it conducted by “a wild animal”. Moreover, did I cry or get sad over a little matter like “that of a child?”

Don’t get angry of other people without proper reason, in fact, there is almost never a good reason to start a terrible argument to aggravate a situation.
