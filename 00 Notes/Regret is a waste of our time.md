# Regret is a waste of our time

> “Epictetus tells us that regret is a waste of our emotional energy. We cannot change the past–it is outside of our control. We can, and should, learn from it.”

We cannot change the past, it is just something out of our [[We cannot control the past|control]]. Therefore, it is a “waste of our emotional energy” to feel worked up on a dilemma that we cannot change.

**How to apply IRL?**

- You will make decisions that you later regret
- After you have made the decision it is almost always impossible to reverse that specific decision
- Therefore, you cannot feel mad or annoyed at yourself for doing that.
