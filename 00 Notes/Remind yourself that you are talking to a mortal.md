# Remind yourself that you are talking to a mortal

>”if you kiss your child, or brother, or friend … you must remind yourself that you love a mortal, and that nothing that you can love is your very own; it is given you for the moment, not for ever.”

Clear realisation that everyone you meet, everyone you love or admire is a [[Mortality|mortal]]. So nomatter the pressure, don’t feel too sad or mourn when someone leaves, as they are “given you for the moment, not for ever.”

A hard concept but it is reality.
