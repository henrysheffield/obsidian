# Remove the judgement 'I am hurt'

> “Remove the judgement, and you have removed the thought ‘I am hurt’: remove the thought ‘I am hurt’, and the hurt itself is removed.”
> *4.7*

Pain is a matter of perception, temporary and inside the mind.

## Apply to:

**Cold showers** 
Just remove the inside judgement ‘It is cold’ or ‘I don’t like this’
Once you move past this, you are invincible.

**Muay thai**
Kicking the bag and it stings? Don’t care, it is temporary pain, remove the judgement that *it stings*, instead just kick the bag as hard, powerful and as fast as you can possibly do it.
