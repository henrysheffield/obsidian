# Ryan Holiday's 3-Step System for Reading Like a Pro

![Ryan Holiday's 3-Step System for Reading Like a Pro](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gT1EExZkzMM)


## Step 1: Read the book

- Ryan uses a real book, according to him, we spend too long looking at screens
- Actively highlights and make notes
- Read slowly–speed reading is bullshit

## Step 2: Physical Flashcards

- Comes back to book after 1 - 2 weeks
- Creates flashcards (physically) with tags, quote, questions
- Puts them into a massive box, sorts them and review them
