# "Every hour of the day give vigorous attention … As it was the last day of your life"

*Meditations - 2.5*

“As it were the last of your life” - Marcus strongly insists that every action, aim, impulse or thought should be important with a clear, well thought outcome.

“To the performance of the task in hand with precise analysis, with unaffected dignity, with human sympathy, with dispassionate justice.” - Focus deeply on every task, no matter how simple it is.

## Essay

At the beginning of *Book 2* of Marcus Aurelius’ journal, *Meditations* he strongly insists himself to give every task at hand powerful and strong attention. It is largely believed that Aurelius wrote *Meditations* at approximately 49 to 59 years of age, leaving a constant lingering sense of mortality throughout the book [^1]. 

Nevertheless, he managed to write the following passage, “Every hour of the day give vigorous attention.” (2.5 - Meditations) The statement showcases his persistance in every hour of the day[^2]–insisting that we must acknowledge that life is short, and make the most of the day. 

Furthermore, Aurelius writes, “As it were the last of your life.” Once again, strongly insisting that every task, goal, aim, thought or impulse must have a clear and well thought outcome. For example, imagine that you have just received an assessment notification for Issues Studies at school. Personally, I do not enjoy the subject. However, according to Aurelius, it is essential that I take into account the significance of any test, like it was the ‘last one of my life.' The best possible outcome is for me to begin preparing straight away: create a calendar event, study 5 minutes tonight, write everything I should know for the future on a sticky note and place it onto my laptop. 

Ultimately, Aurelius had a constant lingering sense of mortality through the text, and strongly advised himself to treat each hour of the day preciously. Moreover, he dedicated himself to carrying out each task with upmost strength. I can apply this concept to my studies at school and my daily tasks every day.
