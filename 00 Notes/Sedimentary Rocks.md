# Sedimentary Rocks

## Notes

- From *seddimentum* in latin, meaning *settling*
- Formed by accumulation, compression and cementation of sediments from other particles and matter. ([[Rock Cycle]])
- These rocks make a significant amount of Earth’s layers (e.g. in [[Lithosphere]])
- Have noticeable layers called *strata*

Crucial for scientists as they act as a geological timeline which also contain fossils.
