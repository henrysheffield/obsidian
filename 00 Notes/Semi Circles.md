# Semi Circles

## Notes

General form: $$y = k \pm\sqrt{r^2-(x-h)^2}$$
Where
$r$ - radius
$(h,k)$ - center
$\pm$ - plus means positive semicircle, likewise negative

General form for side circles (left or right) $$x=h \pm \sqrt{r^2-(y-k)^2}$$
Right half: $x = h+\sqrt{r^2-(y-k)^2}$
Left half: $x = h-\sqrt{r^2-(y-k)^2}$

4 different types
