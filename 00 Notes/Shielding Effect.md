# Shielding Effect

## Notes

- Valence electrons attracted to nucleus
- Inner shell electrons act as a repulsion force against the valence electrons
- Causes changes in
	- Atomic radius (size)
	- Ionisation energy (how easy it is to take a valence electron)

**Increases down a group (atoms gain more inner electron shells)**

**Constant across a period, electrons are added to outer shell but inner shell electrons remain constant**
