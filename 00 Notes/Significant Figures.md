# Significant Figures

## Notes

- Leading zeros not significant ($0.003$→1)
- Zeros between numbers ARE significant ($503$→3)
- Trailing zeros are only significant if decimal place ($5030$→3 but $503.0$→4)
- In exams, keep full calculator value until final answer, then you answer **in correct significant figures**
### Examples

$$4.6 \times 3.52 = 16.192$$
Lowest significant figures is $4.6$→2, so answer should match
$$4.6 \times 3.52\approx16 \quad \text{Correct Significant Figures (2)}$$

$$96.752 \div 3.541 = 27.3234$$
$3.541$ has 4 significant figures → answer should match
$$96.752 \div 3.541 \approx 27.32 \quad \text{Correct Significant Figures (4)}$$
