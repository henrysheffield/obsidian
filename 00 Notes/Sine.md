# Sine

## Notes

- Tracks vertical height of a point $(\cos \theta, \sin \theta)$

$$domain \in \mathbb{R},range \in [-1,1]$$
range is locked in at -1 to 1, visualise by either the [[Unit Circle|unit circle]] where the radius is one, or the sine graph *(see below)* where the amplitude is a value of 1.

Period = $360^{\circ}$ or $2 \pi$
Symmetric ($\sin(- \theta)=-\sin (\theta)$)
Starts at $(0,0)$

$$\sin \theta = \frac{opposite}{hypotenuse}$$
(Remember this by ==SOH== CAH TOA)
