# Sleep Routine

## Notes

| **Time**          | **Activity**                                                                    |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **18:00 - 19:00** | Dinner (or before) + dessert (ideally yogurt + berries but sometimes ice cream) |
| **19:30**         | Warm shower                                                                     |
| **19:50**         | **Screens Off** (Strictly)                                                      |
| **20:00 - 20:45** | Relaxing activity (Reading, light stretching, meditation)                       |
| **20:45 - 21:00** | Lights out / Get into bed - The goal is to go to sleep before 10:00             |
| **06:00**         | Wake up                                                                         |
## Tips

- Have a shower minimum 60+ minutes before bed
- Screens off 60+ minutes before bed
- Try to eat food 3-4 hours before sleeping, this improves your sleep
- If you do eat a high sugar dessert, do a minimum of 3 hours before sleeping
