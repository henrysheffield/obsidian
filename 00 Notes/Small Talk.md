# Small Talk

## Notes

- Scrap any personal benefit of the conversation and just enjoy talking
- Ask Open-ended questions
- Perform [[Active Listening]] by actually listening and comprehending what the other person is saying
- Make the other person feel values / special just by listening to whatever they do.

> [!QUOTE]
>  People will forget experiences, words, sights **but not how they felt around you**

> [!TIP]
> Who cares what other people think of you? Everyone is mortal, we will all die one day, so why not do something crazy today? 
> Core theme in: [[Meditations]]
### 3 Question Rule to ask before talking to someone

1. Whats the worst that could happen?
2. Will I see this person again?
	No? → Who cares
3. Will I regret not talking?
