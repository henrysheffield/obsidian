# Small events are nothing to be ashamed of

>”Crates had Zeno go around with a potful of lentil soup. Crates then broke the pot, and Zeno took to flight in shame, with his teacher yelling after him, in full view of a crowd: “Why run away, my little Phoenician? Nothing terrible has befallen you.” - pp.18


Small events are nothing to get angry, distressed or ashamed of. This reflects that [[Dichotomy|not everything is in our control]]
