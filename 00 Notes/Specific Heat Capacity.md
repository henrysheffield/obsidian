# Specific Heat Capacity

## Notes

- Amount of energy required to heat a substance by $1^{\circ}C$ or $1~K$. It measures a material’s ability to store energy

$$Q = mc \Delta T$$
*where
m = mass (kg)
c = specific heat capacity of substance ($J / kg^{\circ}C$)
$\Delta T$ = Change in temperature (Celsius / [[Kelvin]])


## Examples / Problems

How much energy needed to heat $500 g$ of water at $20^{\circ}C$ to $50^{\circ}C$ in kJ? :: c = 4.18 J / g C → $$Q = mc \Delta T = (500g)(4.18J / g^{\circ}C)(30^{\circ}C)=62700J=62.7kJ$$
