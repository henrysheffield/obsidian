# Staphylococcus Aureus

## Description

Staphylococcus aureus is a [[Gram Positive Bacteria]] spherically shaped bacterium, a member of the Bacillota, and is a usual member of the microbiota of the body, frequently found in the upper respiratory tract and on the skin. [^1] 

## Key Ideas

- Type of germ that ~30% of people carry in noses [^2]
- Can sometimes cause infections, otherwise harmless
- Some types are resistant to [[Antibiotics]]
- Infections can be: pimples, boils or other skin conditions
