# Stoicism An In-Depth Explanation

![Stoicism: An In-Depth Explanation](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ggkd_uyDqz8&t=952s)

## Notes

- Peripatetics = followers of Aristotle
- All greek schools were searching for the *eudaimonic* life–the best way to live life
- Cynics are more intense Stoicism e.g. it’s ok to be wealthy → become poor on purpose
- Cynic = dog in ancient greek, depicts what people thought of Cynics
- Chryssipus = person who systemized Stoicism, few remnants of his work remain

**Field analogy to Stoicism**
The universe is a large field.
Around the field is logic
The trees and plants are physics
The fruit which grows from the trees are ethics

### Stoic Physics

- Everything has a body
- Metaphysics = the study of reality, underlying principles of the universe
- Belived in Aristotle view that the universe is made of fire, water, air and earth
- Divides things into:
**1. Active (Universal Logos?)**
- The bigger picture
- Pushes passive objects to do things
**2. Passive (Individual Logos?)**
- us?
- regular things

**Logos = word**
- logos is a rational entity
- In fact, a bible entry says: *It first began with the Word*
- the word being Logos, reference to Stoicism
- The world goes through periods of disorder (which is fire) and times of peace (e.g. *pax romanica*)
- Universe is highly deterministic 

### Logic

- Dialectics? - Knowledge of what is true, false and either true OR false

**Stoic Sage**
- Perfect human who has achieved flawless virtue
- Emotionalness
- Applies virtues to every opportunity
- Impossible, but is possible to work towards
- Sage applies the universe’s following nature principle in every decision

### Ethics

- To act virtuously, we must first understand human and our own nature
- Ethics is achieved by following nature, that is following the virtues

[[The Four Virtues of Stoicism]]
- Courage - bravery, standing up for what you believe in, embracing new experiences
- Wisdom - Constantly gaining and sharing knowledge
- Temperance - Balancing out everything, including the other virtues
- Justice - Respecting others well, even those who have done wrong to you

- Aurelius pities others who do wrong, even when the wrong is directed at him. This is because Aurelius believes that these people are ignorant and fail to see the damage that they are causing (*amathia*)

> [!QUOTE] 
> “The best revenge is not to be like your enemy.” - Meditations

- Wealth, status and pleasure make life easier and more enjoyable but are not the ultimate goals

### Roman Stoics

- Aurelius (an emperor)
- Epictetus (a slave)
- Socrates (a statesman)
- Gaius Musonius Rufus (teacher of Socrates)
- Everyone could perform stoicism

### Modern Stoicism

- Removes the odd parts of stoicism (especially the physics)
- Takes the ethics and discipline, of which we can apply in our everyday life
- Key people:
	- Ryan Holiday
	- Massimo Puglicci
	- others
