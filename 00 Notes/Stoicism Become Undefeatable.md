## Stoicism: Become Undefeatable

![](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EFkyxzJtiv4)

### Zeno of Citium

Zeno was the founder and primary teacher of Stoicism. Around 300 BCE, he lost his entire ship and all of his wealth to a devastating shipwreck, instantly plunging him into poverty due to the forces of nature.

Because there was absolutely nothing that Zeno could do to reverse the situation, he chose to remain neutral and *indifferent* to the pain. Even though his circumstances became much worse, he pushed forward without anger or despair. ==He recongised that wasting energy protesting unchangeable reality was futile==

**Further Reading:** [Zeno’s Shipwreck](Zeno’s Shipwreck)
    

### The Core Idea: The [[Dichotomy]] of Control

We do not have control over external events, but we have absolute control over how we react to them.

#### The Analogy of Rain

- **The Reality:** Rain is essential for the Earth, allowing plants to grow and life to form.
    
- **The Reaction:** People generally feel annoyed or downcast when there is rain compared to sunshine.
    
- **The Stoic Lesson:** We do not cry over the rain because we have accepted it as a natural occurrence outside of our control. We have adapted to this "misfortune" and remain largely neutral. Stoicism is the practice of applying this exact same neutrality to all external events.
