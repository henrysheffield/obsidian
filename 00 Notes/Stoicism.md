# Stoicism

## Notes

### Core Idea

*We cannot control everything BUT we can control how we react to everything.*

→Apply to all situations

Began in Athens, Greece around the year $300~BCE$ by Zeno of Citium (a phonenician merchant)

Theory based largely on the [[Dichotomy]] of Control - Meaning that life is divided into two things:

1. What you can control (your effort, your motivation, how you react to things)
2. What you can’t control (your exam results, others’ opinions of you, the weather)

Stoicism is a way to approach life–largely i”the discipline of actionn what I believe as not complaining. Stoics believe that what you cannot control, you must accept that you cannot control it, and never get mad or sad–especially not lash out as others.

One of the underlying goals of this philosophy is to be indifferent to pain and [[Pleasure Principle|pleasure]].


## Examples / Problems

Consider the following scenario: imagine you have been working hard for the past 6 months, eager to get a promotion[^1]. In a stoic’s point of view, if the boss does not give you a raise, it is useless and a waste of emotional energy[^2]
