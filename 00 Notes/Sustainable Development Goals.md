# Sustainable Development Goals

## Notes

17 Goals proposed by the UN to achieve before 2030. Includes: **end poverty, famine, sustainability, climate change, etc**

---
## Examples

### SDG 11: Sustainable Cities and Communities

This goal aims to make cities and human settlements **safe, sustainable, inclusive and resilient**
→Improving the public transport system by utilising **electric buses:** benefits social and economic.

> [!QUOTE]
> *Up to 3 billion people struggle to afford housing*
> - UN Website - 2025 - SDG 11
### SDG 7: Affordable and clean energy

Ensure access to **clean, modern, reliable and affordable** energy for all.

### SDG 12: Ensure sustainable consumption and production patterns

Transition from a **linear economy** (take-make-dispose) to a **circular economy** (take-make-reuse)
→All businesses should prioritise making biodegradable products to contribute to the **circular economy**
→*Real life example:* Kathmandu creates a biodegradable puffer vest
### SDG 13: Climate action

- Urgent action to combat climate change
- [[Greenhouse Effect]]
- 2024 was hottest year on record–global temperature reached $1.55^{\circ} C$ higher than pre-industrial levels. (SDG Website)

An urgent action to combat the massive threat of [[Climate Change]].
→Countries should transition from fossil fuels to renewables and clean energy.

> [!QUOTE]
> **2024 was hottest year** on record global temperature, reaching $1.55^{\circ} C$ higher than pre-industrial levels.[^1]
