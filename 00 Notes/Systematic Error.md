# Systematic Error

## Notes

A consistent, repeatable flaw in an experiment which skews the data to a certain value (e.g. 2 units too high).

Caused by a flaw in the experiment, usually a flaw in the experimental design, faulty equipment or incorrect calibration.

This error affects [[Accuracy|accuracy]] of the experiment but the [[Precision|precision]] can still remain high.
## Examples / Problems

Using a ruler with the first cm missing, you can take 100 measurements but it doesn’t matter, your results will always be one cm higher than they should be.
