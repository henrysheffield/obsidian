# Tan

## Notes

Represents slope of the line tangent to the [[Unit Circle]]

$$\tan \theta = \frac{\sin \theta}{\cos \theta}$$
Domain →$\mathbb{R} \setminus {cos \theta}$
Range → $\mathbb{R}$
Period: $180^{\circ}$ or $\pi$
Symmetry (odd function): $\tan(-\theta) = -\tan(\theta)$
