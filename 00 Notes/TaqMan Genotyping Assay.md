# TaqMan Genotyping Assay

## Notes

Real time ([[qPCR]])  method to identify [[Single Nucleotide Polymorphisms (SNPs)]] and other genetic variations.

![[TaqMan Genotyping Assay-1784672862911.webp]]

1. DNA strand separates
2. Primers connect to specific parts of the gene
3. [[Probe]] specifically goes in between target sequence of amplicon
4. Polymerase eats probe → probe undergoes *cleavage* which breaks it apart
5. Reporter and quencher and separated → reporter emits florescent light
6. Information recorded, meaning that there has been an amplification of the DNA
