# Tectonic Plates

## Notes

- Like puzzle pieces of the Earth
- Massive, irregular shape rocks that form Earth’s [[Lithosphere]]
- 7 Major plates (*Pacific, North American, Eurasian, etc*)
- >12 more smaller plates
- The plates are constantly shifting (about 1 - 1.5 cm per year due to small [[Convection]] currents in Earth’s mantle.
