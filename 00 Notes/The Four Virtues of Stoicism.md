# The Four Virtues of Stoicism

## Notes

Stoicism divides itself into four main virtues.

The aim is to use them in every opportunity, every day

### 1. Courage

- Arguably the most important
- Stand up for what you believe in
- Never be a coward
- Example: In a group task if you realise you receive more work than you should, stand up for yourself without getting mad. Present your arguments clearly and quickly. [[he is also ready to accept a possible negative outcome with equanimity|Be ready to accept a failure as well.]]

### 2. Justice

- Do the right thing
- Treat others with respect
- It doesn’t matter if another has done wrong, treat *everybody* with kindness and sympathy

>[!QUOTE]
>’Do the right thing, the rest doesn’t matter’
>Marcus Aurelius


### 3. Temperance

- Balance
- Not too much of anything
- Right amount of other virtues
- Example: If you are having a delicious dinner, never eat yourself until you are grotesquely full, just near the ‘full’ mark instead

### 4. Wisdom

- Education
- Constant pursuit of knowledge
- Example: Keep being passionate of what you are learning, whether it be biology, chemistry, philosophy, maths. **Do what you want to do**
