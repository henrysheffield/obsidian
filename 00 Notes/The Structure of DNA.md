*My sketch of a nucleotide*

A [[Nucleotide]] is made of: a phosphate group, a sugar and a nitrogenous base (ACTG)
Because it is missing a hydroxyl group → deoxy

The sequence in [[DNA]] is caused by bonds of the phosphate group on $3'$

- Each base forms Hydrogen bond with a complementary base pair (`AT` and `CG` or `TA` and `GC`)
- Thymine and Cytosine are pyrimidines (single ring structure)
- Adenine and Guanine are purines (double rings)
- The turn of DNA is highly regular, with about 10 base pairs per turn
- Major and minor group are called grooves (had no idea what is happening here!)

- Adenine bonds with thymine, forming 2 hydrogen bonds
- Guanine bonds with cytosine, forming 3 hydrogen bonds
