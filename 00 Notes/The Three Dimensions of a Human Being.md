# The Three Dimensions of a Human Being

## Notes

*According to [[Logotherapy]]*

### 1. The Somatic (Body)

- Physical, and biological layer
- Includes: bodily systems, genetics, reflexes

### 2. The Psychic (Mind)

- The psychological and emotional layer
- Learned behaviors, emotions, habits

### 3. The [[noölogical]] (Spirit)

- Human core
- Dimension of freedom, responsibility, love, humor art, deliberate pursuit of purpose

> [!TIP]
> The body and mind (somatic and psychic) can become sick, but the spirit remains intact. Relation: [[He who has a why to live for can bear almost any how]]
