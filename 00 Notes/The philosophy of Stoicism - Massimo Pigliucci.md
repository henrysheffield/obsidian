![The philosophy of Stoicism - Massimo Pigliucci](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=R9OCA6UFE-0&t=51s)

## Notes

### Zeno of Citium 
- Lost everything
- Instead of being depressed, he walked into a bookstore and began to read *Socrates*
### Stoicism

- Stoics don’t search for the best society; instead, they try to make the most of the society right now as that is in their control
- We can harness our own self control and will power to make our and others’ situation better

### [[The Four Virtues of Stoicism]]

Wisdom - Navigate complex situations in a logicial manner
Temperance - Keeping everything in balance
Justice - Treating others fairly even when they have done wrong
Courage - Facing daily challenges calmy, orderly and well (efficiently)
