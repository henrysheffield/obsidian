# The prisoner who lost faith in the future - was doomed

> “The prisoner who had lost faith in the future–his future–was doomed … There he remained, lying in his own excreta, and nothing bothered him more.”
> pp.82

Without meaning, hope, the situation becomes and feels pointless. At this point, the prisoner would die, rather than dying from a disease, torture or killing would die from lack of hope.
