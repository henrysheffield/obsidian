# There will always be assholes in the world. Ignore and respect them.

> “Say to yourself first thing in the morning: today I shall meet people who are meddling, ungrateful, aggressive, treacherous, malicious, unsocial.” 
> 
> *2.1*

It is inevitable that you will meet people which you loath. Because it is out of your control, you must adapt and understand this concept. Simply ignore them but maintain a level of respect–never lash out at people.
