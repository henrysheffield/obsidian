# Things in the mind

> “First, that *things* cannot touch the mind: they are external and inert; anxieties can only come from your own moral judgement.”

Some *things* are real and can cause pain (e.g. a knife). But there a more minor things that we make a big deal out of (e.g. non-optimal weather). If you say ‘I’m not cold’ then you are not cold, granted it is not snowing.
