# Think before you act (Epictetus)

> “we should act in order, with due consideration, and with proper care.” 
> Epictetus - pp.23


According to Epictetus, we should “act in order,” conveying the message of undertaking every [[No action undertaken without aim|action purposefully with a clear aim]] to ensure you complete your goals. In real life, we can combat procastination by acting in order e.g. shutting your laptop the second you feel lazy or unproductive- → lie on beanbag and read a book / go for a walk and listen to music.
