# Transcription

## Notes

The biological process where a specific segment of [[DNA]] (a [[Gene]]) is copied into [[RNA|messenger RNA (mRNA)]] by the [[Enzyme|enzyme]] **RNA Polymerase**.

### 1. RNA Polymerase binds to a specific sequence in the gene, named the ‘promoter’ 

The promoter tells RNA Polymerase where to start the transcription process in the gene.

### 2. Pries two strands apart

RNA Polymerase ‘unzips’ the gene

### 3. Elongation (RNA Polymerase moves down the gene)

RNA Polymerase synthesises mRNA as it goes.
Reads in direction 3’ →5’ but mRNA is built 5’ →3’
Zips DNA back as it goes

### 4. Termination

RNA Polymerase leaves
mRNA leaves, outside of the nucleus, and finds a [[Ribosome]]
DNA back to original state

ALSO:
Cap at 5’ end
Poly-A polymerase enzyme caps the 3’ end of mRNA strand
These caps protect the mRNA from being degraded by other enzymes
