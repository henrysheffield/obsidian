## Transition Metals

### Summary

> [!ABSTRACT] Description
> Elements that are located in the d-block of the Periodic Table (groups 3 - 12). They can lose different amount of electrons causing different charges.
> → $Fe^{2+} \quad Fe^{3+}$
> 

### Key Ideas

- **Groups 3 - 12** - Located in the d-block of the periodic table. They are all metals.
    
- Can lose different amount of electrons due to an incomplete valence shell

### Properties

- High melting/boiling points
- High conductivity
- Exhibit multiple oxidation states
- Can lose different amounts of electrons

### Naming

When bonding a transition and a non metal, you must use the formula: $$\text{Transition Metal + (Charge) + Non metal + (Suffix: -ide)}$$
> [!QUESTION] **Name:** $CuCl_{2}$
> Copper (II) Chloride

### Flashcards

Describe what transition metals are? :: Transition elements (also known as transition metals) are elements that have partially filled d orbitals. IUPAC defines transition elements as **_an element having a d subshell that is partially filled with electrons, or an element that has the ability to form stable cations with an incompletely filled d orbital._**
