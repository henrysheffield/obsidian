# Translation

## Notes

Biological process where cells use genetic instructions in messenger [[RNA]] (mRNA) to build [[Proteins|proteins]]. The second step after [[Transcription]]

I would strongly recommending watching an animation to comprehend the idea further.

![](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gG7uCskUOrA)

### 1. mRNA leaves nucleus, finds a [[Ribosome]]

Ribosome binds to mRNA, reads the code in mRNA to begin producing an amino acid chain to later form a protein.
### 2. Process

Ribosome reads 3 bases at a time
Each time, a [[RNA#3. Transport RNA (tRNA)|transport RNA (tRNA)]] delivers the corresponding amino acid which is added to a growing chain of amino acids.
Once a stop codon comes, the process it finished and the protein leaves to the [[Golgi Apparatus]] to be further modified
