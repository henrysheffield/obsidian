# Book Note

> “Our friends who had thought they were travelling to freedom that night had been taken in the trucks to this camp, and there they were locked in the huts and burned to death.”
> pp.70

Frankl was originally going to go with his friends in the truck but decided to follow fate and remain where he was.

This event shows the uncertainty, chance and misfortune of world war 2.
