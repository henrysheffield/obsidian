# Twelve Angry Men Quotes

## Notes

*”I’ve lived with ‘em all my life. They tell nothing but lies. You know that. They’re born liars.” - Juror 10*

- Shows prejudice
- Hostile bigotry 
- Xenophobia
- Even chauvinism?

*”Rotten kid. You work your heart out.* - Juror 3

- Demonstrates Juror 3’s own anger against his estranged son
- He doesn’t see the defendant, he views the boy as his own son

*”It is always the most difficult thing to keep prejudice out of a thing like this. And whenever it occurs, prejudice always obscures truth.”* - Juror 8

- Illustrates the challenge of prejudice
- Rose uses a brilliant metaphor of “*obscures truth*” to indicate how prejudice acts as a fog, disillusioning the jurors from seeing reasonable doubt and truth.

*”We’re talking about someone’s life here. We can’t decide it in 5 minutes. Suppose that we’re wrong?* - Juror 8

- Importance of reasonable doubt
==- Juxtaposition of a tiny time frame of “5 minutes” and “someone’s life” cause deep realisation of the severity of this case==

*”Human life have a value? Supposing it were your boy? Supposing it were you?” - Juror 11*

- Immigrant who has witnessed facism
- Deep respect for legal system.
