## Flashcards

What is the union in probability? :: Symbolises the set of A, B and both A and B. Represented by a smiley face. ($A \cup B$)
<!--SR:!2026-06-25,12,270-->

Difference between union and intersection in a venn diagram? :: Union ($A \cup B$) represents both circles and the inside - meaning A,B and the intersection. While the intersection ($A \cap B$) only represents where A and B are equal.
