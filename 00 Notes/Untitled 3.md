DNA is made of: a phosphate group, a deoxyribose sugar and a nitrogenous base (adenine, guanine, cytosine or guanine)

Polymerase chain reaction is a laboratory technique to amplify the amount of a specific DNA sample.

It works by first heating up the sample to 95 degrees celsius, this separates the alpha helix into two strands

Next, the annealing process is when the sample is cooled to ~55 degrees and primerss bind to specific parts of the DNA strands

Next, the substance is heated/ and TAQ / DNA Polymerase binds to the primer and replicates the DNA–doubling it. 

This technique allows scientists to mass produce a strand of DNA
