Logos is the deity in stoicism.

SToicism says that we live in a pantheistic universe, meaning that the universe and us are all part of one, intelligent, coherent and rational entity. 

We must align our thoughts with the logos as there is a small fragment of it in all of us.

We can achieve this by following nature / fate 

Excample: VIktor Frankl let fate guide him, he didn’t purposefully go to Aushwitz, instead he remained in his homeland Austria, letting fate guide his path rather than choosing his own way.
