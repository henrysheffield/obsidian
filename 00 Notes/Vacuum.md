# Vacuum

## Notes

- A physical place that does not contain any matter at all
- Causes the gas pressure to be significantly less than atmospheric pressure
- Impossible for a perfect vacuum (meaning: no atoms inside)
- **Basically:** a volume containing little to no matter
- Extremely low pressure
- comes from latin word *vaccus* meaning *empty*
