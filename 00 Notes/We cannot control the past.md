# We cannot control the past

>”Epictetus tells us that regret is a waste of our emotional energy. We cannot change the past – it is outside of our control. ==We can, and should learn from it.”==[^1]

### Concept 1: Regret

According to [[Epictetus]], “regret is a waste of emotional energy,” which I completely agree, we cannot change the past, so what is the use of [[If you cannot control everything, what is the use of being gloomy?|gloomy about past events out of your control?]]

### Concept 2: Learning from our mistakes

This is the most crucial part of anything in life

→You just got a math test back where you failed the test. 

*What do you do?* 

**You do not feel angry or sad about the results, that is a waste of time and you are much better off to immediately search for your mistakes and ensure that you never make them again (essential for exams)**

[^1]: Very crucial part of the past, we must always learn from our mistakes to improve.
