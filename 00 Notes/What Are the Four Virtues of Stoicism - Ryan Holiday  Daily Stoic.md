# What Are the Four Virtues of Stoicism - Ryan Holiday  Daily Stoic

![What Are the Four Virtues of Stoicism? | Ryan Holiday | Daily Stoic](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yaG7f3uivPU)

## Notes

These are the virtues to live by–it doesn’t get better than this. We must apply these principles into every single opportunity everyday.

### 1. Courage

- Put yourself out there
- Stand up for what you believe in
- No cowardness

### 2. Justice

- Do the right thing
- How you treat others

> [!QUOTE]
> “Do the right thing, the rest doesn’t matter.’ 
> Marcus Aurelius - 6.2

### 3. Temperance

- Balance
- Do things the correct way (e.g. no shortcuts in reading–speed reading is false)
- Right amount of other virtues (you cannot be ultra smart in wisdom but have no courage in showing your wisdom)

### 4. Wisdom

- Education
- Constant pursuit of knowledge 
## Application

- [ ] Every situation where you can apply these virtues, write it down in a note / your journal straight away, you can look back on it in the future.
