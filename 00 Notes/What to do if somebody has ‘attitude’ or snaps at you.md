## Notes

> [!QUOTE]
> “Or perhaps a coworker has snapped at you, or someone else. Rather than hurling (or mumbling to yourself) think, ‘Have I ever snapped at someone? Yes of  course.”
> [[How To Be A Stoic]] - pp.228

The next time somebody has attitude, do not even “mumble to yourself,” but instead think about when you acted the same way. This is an easy way to prevent anger and increase [[Respect]]
