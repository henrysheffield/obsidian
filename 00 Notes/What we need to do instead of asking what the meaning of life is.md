# Book Note

>”Life ultimately means taking the responsibility to finding the right answer to its problems and to fulfill the tasks which it constantly sets for each individual.”
>pp.85

**What is most important:**
It is not searching for a meaning of life

**Instead:**
- Fulfilling our daily tasks
- Focusing on what matters to us (ie. exams, french, family, self-improvement)
- Actively work on these goals

**Do you think Frankl wanted to get up every morning to work?**
No, but he took the responsibility which fate gave him, and carried out the work.
No complaining, no giving up, just arriving and doing the work given to him.
