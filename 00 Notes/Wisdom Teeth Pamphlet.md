# Wisdom Teeth Pamphlet

## Notes

[[Mandible]] = largest, strongest and only moveable bone in human skull

**Why do we have wisdom teeth?**

Humans originally needed wisdom teeth to grind tough, fibrous foods. As we have learned to cook and had softer foods, our mouths have evolved to be smaller. This leaves no room for the wisdom teeth which can cause problems.

- Saltwater kills some bacteria and temporarily inhibits growth of other bacteria
- Abscess = pocket of pus, sign of bacterial infection

**Why is it better to remove wisdom teeth in youths?**

- In young people, a tooth’s roots have not formed totally → bone surrounding tooth is softer → easier removal of tooth, less risk of damage

[[Orthodontics]] - literally means ‘straight teeth’ in greek

- Swelling almost always occurs after surgery and can vary from little to severe
- To reduce swelling, you may be administered [[Corticosteriods]] (synthetic drugs with similar effects to [[Cortisol]]
- Studies show that ice packs do not reduce swelling, but may relieve pain and provide some comfort
- After surgery **rest**, no muay thai for 2 - 4 weeks

- Confused by the different types of [[Anaesthesia]]
		Local: A specific part of the body is numb, you are fully awake but zero pain
		Conscious sedation: You are in a very sleepy state and semi-conscious
		General: Fully asleep, you have zero consciousness
- For the first day after surgery, do not rinse out your mouth or spit with force as this can loosen the blood clot and may slow healing
**Excessive bleeding side effect (haemorrhage)** - Uncommon but can occur. place a gauze pack over wound and bite gently on gauze for 15 - 30 minutes. DO NOT USE COTTON WOOL OR TISSUES TO BITE ON.
