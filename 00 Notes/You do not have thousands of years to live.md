# You do not have thousands of years to live

> “No, you do not have thousands of years to live. Urgency is on you. While you live, while you can, become good.” - 4.17

A clear [[Mortality]] theme, exemplifies the urgent need to achieve great things.

Also has a [[Kindness]] theme of “becoming good”
