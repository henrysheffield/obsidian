Question :: Write ionic formula for the compound: aluminium sulfide
Mistake :: $Al_{2}(SO_{4})_{3}$
Correction :: $Al_{2}S_{3}$
Explanation :: I mistaked sulfate ($SO_{4}^{2-}$) and Sulfide ($S^{2-}$)

> [!ABSTRACT] Extra Info
> Sulfate is a polyatomic ion while sulfide is just the ion
