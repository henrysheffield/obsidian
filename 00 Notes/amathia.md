# Note

## Notes

*Amathia* is an ancient greek term meaning:

Opposite of wisdom, a kind of dis-knowledge of how to deal with other human beings, which results in awful actions undertaken by otherwise perfectly functional human beings.

These human beings cannot be persuaded by reasonable argument.
