## Notes

> [!QUOTE]
> “Thus achieve what the Stoics called *ataraxia* or tranquillity of mind.”
> [[How To Be A Stoic]] - pp.10

Lucid state of calmness. tranquillity and freedom from disturbance.

Similar to the *nirvana* in Buddhism
