# he is also ready to accept a possible negative outcome with equanimity

>”But he is also ready to accept a possible negative outcome with equanimity.”
>35

Edit: BEFORE you begin a task with a negative potential outcome, acknowledge that you may fail, but accept the real fact anyway.

Whenever you do something with a very real potential negative outcome (e.g. DELF B2) be prepared of the fact of the possible bad result. Accept whatever you get with “equanimity.”
