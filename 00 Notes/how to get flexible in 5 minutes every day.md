#  how to get flexible in 5 minutes every day

**Quick Summary:** This method said: be consistent (realistically do 10 - 20 minutes per day). Focus on only a few types of stretches. Perform PNF stretching, intense but short sessions.

![](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P8tYVm0PeNE)

---
## Captured Notes

- Focus on the following stretches
	- Pike (head-to-feet, pull yourself in to your body)
	- Pancake (sort of side and you lean forward)
	- Front Split (use a pillow for knee and do PNF)
		→ Try to aim for 10 reps each side
		→ Focus on pushing your back knee into the ground rather than the front leg
	- Side Split (This takes awhile)
		→ You need to practice pigeon stretch for rotation
		→ You must lean forward to enable your bone joint to move
	- Pigeon Stretch
	- Full Bridge 

## Synthesis & Key Ideas

**Concept 1:** PNF Stretching
	- This is a method of gaining flexibility that is the best way to get flexible rapidly
	- You put yourself into an intense stretch for a short period and relax and repeat
