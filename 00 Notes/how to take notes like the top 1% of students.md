#  how to take notes like the top 1% of students

**Quick Summary:** Gohar describes that you should process information from a lecturer, let it digest, take notes *in your own words* and revisit and revise the notes within 24 hours, then performing spaced repetition for the next few months.

---
##  Captured Notes

- Do not copy words one-for-one instead read / listen to a lecturer, let the information absorb for 10-20 seconds and write it in your own words
- Use comparisons, recall and relations
- Add question marks to what does not make sense, ask for clarification immediately

##  Synthesis & Key Ideas

**Concept 1:** ==Do not copy words one-for-one==
- Instead, let the information sink and put the idea into your own words
- You can always come back to the facts later

**Concept 2**: Compare and create relations
- If a topic reminds you of something, ==write it down immediately==
- e.g. London dispersion forces are temporary forces caused by instantaneous position of electrons in a molecule, this relates to the Heisenberg principle which basically states that an electron can be at any place at any time and there is uncertainty of where it is.
- This is an amazing method that helps you recall past information whilst making connections

**Concept 3:** Ask questions
- If you find part of a topic weird or not understandable:
	- Add a question mark to that note
	- Ask for clarification from your lecturer ==immediately== you will likely not have access to your lecturer in the future so ask them straight away
- It shouldn't make you feel weird socially, it is totally normal and nobody cares
