# Book Note

From Greek word *noös* meaning mind

This is the spirit of a human. 

Contains:
- Love
- Humor
- Pursuit of meaning 
- Cannot be broken as easily as body and mind

### Self-Detachment (Self-Distancing)

- Uniquely human
- Ability to observe your own thoughts–away from the real world
- Allows you to say: *I am anxious right now, but I am not anxiety.*

### Self-Transcedence

- Ability to look beyond yourself, towards something or someone else
- Frankl argued that real fulfillment is a byproduct of forgetting yourself and pointing your life toward a meaningful goal outside your own ego.

### Noögenic Neurosis

A psychological issue originating from the noögenic dimension of a human. 
Caused by an **existential vacuum (profound sense of meaningless, lack of purpose)**
