# qPCR

## Notes

Realtime [[Polymerase Chain Reaction (PCR)]]. Meaning it monitors the reaction in real time, instead of just an end result of yes/no (e.g. there is a change in nucleotide sequence at the end → quantiative numbers during the experiment)

Quantitatively determine presence of a target sequence / gene in a sample.

![[qPCR-1784673364249.webp]]
*qPCR Graph*

- Fluorescence emitted by reporter (VIC, FAM)
- DNA doubling
- Reaches plateau where reaction is over
- $Ct$ value is number of times to cross a certain threshold
